# Audit of the kinds added or changed on 2026-10-21

Reviewer, 2026-10-21 night. Question per [docs/process.md](../../docs/process.md): would a player
build our pick instead of THE OBVIOUS DESIGN (the straightforward build from the mechanics), is its
size proportionate to what it does, and what share of the mechanics ceiling does it reach (ceilings
C1-C11 are derived in [BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md#ceilings-from-the-code)). Obvious-design block counts
marked "est." are my estimates, not judged builds; each fix below starts by building and measuring
that obvious design (process step 2).

**Verdict counts:** keep 14 · fix 7 · remove 10 (pages to hide until a replacement passes G3).

harvester at 3-60 items/h is a part, not an answer. Per-plant efficiency is fine; the pages answer
a question nobody asked.
