# CIRCUIT: auto-door-lock-v1 auto-door door 2x2, open wait 8 gt, close delay 30 gt, 124 blocks, 12x5x7, robust
# auto-door lock v1 (clk-obs, 2026-10-21): first door with memory, not minimal.
# The door: classic6's four sticky pistons in the door plane (z = 0); on each side one torch below
# them powers the bottom piston (through the block it strongly powers) and the top one (through the
# dust on that block). Inner plates (z = +-1) feed, through the dust under their supports (y = -2),
# a delay-4 repeater A on each side, whose output turns that side's torch off: the door opens.
# Outer plates (z = +-3), one row each way, feed (under their supports) a lock torch on each side:
# while no outer plate is pressed, the lock torch powers a repeater into A's side and A is LOCKED,
# keeping its output. Plates of one kind release in the order they were pressed (each re-checks 20 gt
# after its first press, and a walker crosses one in under 20 gt), so:
# - walking in: the outer plate is pressed (A unlocked), then the inner one (A turns on: door open);
#   the outer plate is released first, then the inner one: A locks while still on (it would take
#   8 gt to turn off; the lock takes 4): the door stays open while someone stands in the doorway;
# - walking out: the inner plate is pressed again (A on), then the outer one (A unlocked); the inner
#   plate is released first: A turns off and the door closes behind the walker; then the outer plate
#   is released and A locks off.
