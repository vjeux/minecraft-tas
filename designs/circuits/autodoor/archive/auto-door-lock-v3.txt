# CIRCUIT: auto-door-lock-v3 auto-door door 2x2, open wait 12 gt, close delay 34 gt, 90 blocks, 8x9x7, robust
# auto-door lock v2 (clk-obs, 2026-10-21): lock-v1 with one drive for the whole door.
# The door is classic6 (four sticky pistons, the frame on top, dust on the frame): the dust's source
# is the top torch of a 3-torch tower beside the door, so the door is powered (closed) exactly when
# the tower's base is not. The base is the output of the lockable delay-4 repeater A fed by the
# inner plates (z = +-1); the outer plates (z = +-3) unlock it through the lock torch (one per door
# now): see lock-v1 for why the door stays open for someone standing in the doorway.
# The far outer row's dust drops under the floor (y = -4) and climbs into the near row's path,
# which points into the lock torch's base.
