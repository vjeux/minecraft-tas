# CIRCUIT: auto-door-lock-v9 auto-door door 2x2, open wait 6 gt, close delay 40 gt, stop in doorway the door stays open, 97 blocks, 7x8x11, robust
# auto-door lock v9 (clk-obs, 2026-10-21): lock-v8 with the lock torch's base beside the near outer
# row: the torch hangs on it (z = 3), and the row's pickup dust turns once and feeds it through the
# 2 gt repeater (2 blocks fewer, the same timings).
# lock-v8: lock-v7 made robust to two walkers at any gap.
# Same door, tower and lock as lock-v7; the outer plate rows move out to z = +-5, four blocks from
# the inner ones, and the timings change: A (inner plates -> door) 2 gt; the lock 12 gt after the
# outer rows let go (a 2 gt repeater into the lock torch's base, the torch, an 8 gt lock repeater).
# Why: every plate lets go on its own 20 gt grid (first press + 20k), so with two walkers the last
# inner plate can let go up to 20 gt after the last walker left it. That walker reaches the end of
# the outer row 4 blocks later (14 gt at a sprint), and the lock waits 12 gt more: 2 + 20 < 14 + 12,
# so A has turned off before the lock catches it. A single walker stopping in the doorway lets go
# of the outer plate 14+ gt before the inner one, so the lock (12 gt) catches A while it is still
# on: 12 < 14 + 2.
