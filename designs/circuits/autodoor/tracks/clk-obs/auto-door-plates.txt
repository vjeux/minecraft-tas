# auto-door-plates (clk-obs, 2026-10-22): THE OBVIOUS AUTOMATIC DOOR, the pick (designs/briefs/auto-door.md;
# track file designs/circuits/autodoor/tracks/clk-obs/obvious-v2.txt). classic6's four sticky pistons in the
# door plane (z = 0), each side powered by a torch below its pistons (the torch strongly powers the stone above
# it, the bottom piston's neighbour, and the dust cross on that stone, which points into the top piston). One
# stone plate row on each side, right next to the door (z = -1, +1); the plates strongly power their supports,
# and the dust under the supports (y = -2) is one network running into both torches' bases: any plate pressed
# turns both torches off and the door opens 2 gt later, before a sprinter reaches it. No memory: someone who
# stops in the doorway presses no plate, and the door closes on them.
