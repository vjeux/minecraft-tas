# CIRCUIT: brewer-1 brewer 1 potions/min per stand 1.9, first potion by 1680 gt, blaze powder 1 for 8 brews, hoppers awake 5, at rest no, a clock runs on, blocks per stand 49.0, bill 40 iron, 3 quartz, 32 redstone, 49 blocks, 5x6x5, robust
# One brewing stand: a clock (two hoppers passing 106 stones back and forth, a copper bulb
# remembering the direction) splits time into two halves of 42 seconds. When the brewing half
# starts, the bottles hopper beside the stand is unlocked, one dropper puts a nether wart into the
# hopper on top of the stand and a second dropper, a moment later, a sugar, which waits there until
# the wart is used up: the stand brews awkward potions, then swiftness. In the other half the
# hopper under the stand takes the three potions to the output chest while the bottles hopper is
# locked (a repeater keeps it locked a moment longer when the halves change, so a fresh bottle is
# never taken out unbrewed). Blaze powder goes into the stand's side from the fuel chest.
