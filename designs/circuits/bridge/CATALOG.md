## gap 3

| design | kind | metrics | blocks | box | robustness | how it works |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [bridge-slime-3](archive/bridge-slime-3.txt) | piston-bridge 3 | gap 3 cells, width 1 cell, extend 3 gt, retract 3 gt, walkway slime block, flips safe (the lever flipped back at any time) | 6 | 3x1x5 | robust | A hidden bridge over a gap of 3 blocks (6 blocks, 3x1x5): flip the lever on and a row of 3 slime blocks slides out of the side wall into the gap; flip it off and the row slides back flush into the wall, leaving the gap open. How it works: one sticky piston behind the middle of the row pushes it, and slime blocks stick together, so the whole row moves as one. The ground at both ends is obsidian, which slime cannot pull (stone there would be dragged back with the row). Keep the other cells next to the slime row empty: slime drags any block it touches. Slime slows a walker a little (pext). |
| [bridge-stone-3](archive/bridge-stone-3.txt) | piston-bridge 3 | gap 3 cells, width 1 cell, extend 3 gt, retract 3 gt, walkway stone, flips safe (the lever flipped back at any time) | 9 | 4x1x5 | robust | A hidden bridge with a stone walkway over a gap of 3 blocks (9 blocks, 4x1x5): flip the lever on and a row of 3 stone blocks slides out of the side wall into the gap; flip it off and the row slides back flush into the wall, leaving the gap open. Stone walks like normal ground (no slime underfoot). How it works: behind the stone row is a row of slime blocks, and behind that one sticky piston. The piston pushes the slime row, which pushes the stone row into the gap; coming back, the slime holds on to the stone and pulls it home (stone does not stick to stone, so only the slime can bring each block back). A piston moves at most 12 blocks, so one piston does up to 6 cells of bridge (pext). |
