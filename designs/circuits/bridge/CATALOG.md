## gap 3

| design | kind | metrics | blocks | box | robustness | how it works |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [bridge-slime-3](archive/bridge-slime-3.txt) | piston-bridge 3 | gap 3 cells, width 1 cell, extend 3 gt, retract 3 gt, walkway slime block, flips safe (the lever flipped back at any time) | 6 | 3x1x5 | robust | A hidden bridge over a gap of 3 blocks (6 blocks, 3x1x5): flip the lever on and a row of 3 slime blocks slides out of the side wall into the gap; flip it off and the row slides back flush into the wall, leaving the gap open. How it works: one sticky piston behind the middle of the row pushes it, and slime blocks stick together, so the whole row moves as one. The ground at both ends is obsidian, which slime cannot pull (stone there would be dragged back with the row). Keep the other cells next to the slime row empty: slime drags any block it touches. Slime slows a walker a little (pext). |
