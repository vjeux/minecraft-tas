# CIRCUIT: bridge-stone-2wide-6 piston-bridge 6 gap 6 cells, width 2 cells, extend 3 gt, retract 3 gt, walkway stone, flips safe (the lever flipped back at any time), 50 blocks, 8x2x9, robust
# A hidden 2-wide bridge with a stone walkway over a gap of 6 blocks (50 blocks, 8x2x9): flip the lever on and
# two rows of 6 stone blocks slide out of the two side walls into the gap, side by side; flip it off and
# both rows slide back flush into their walls, leaving the gap open.
# How it works: each wall holds one stone row with a row of slime blocks behind it and a sticky piston
# behind that (the stone-walkway bridge, mirrored); the two stone rows meet in the middle and stone
# does not stick to stone, so each side pulls only its own row home. One lever powers both pistons
# through a line of redstone dust that runs around the near end of the gap, behind the ground there
# (the dust needs blocks under it) (pext).
