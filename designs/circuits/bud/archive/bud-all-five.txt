# CIRCUIT: bud-all-five bud detects place remove state shape update, latency 1 gt place / 1 gt remove / 1 gt state / 2 gt shape / 1 gt update, pulse 5 gt, re-arm 6 gt, jams place pair 4 gt apart, footprint 4 cells, tileable every 4 (0 0 4), 13 blocks, 3x6x3, robust
name: bud-all-five
circuit: bud
tile: 0 0 4
# Detects all five: the fast slime QC BUD (a stone on the slime block cuts the step between two
# dusts; the piston, armed by quasi-connectivity, takes it off the step in the tick after a block
# update) with its low dust standing on an observer that watches the target below the piston. A
# shape-only change there, which no piston can feel, fires the observer 2 gt later: its back powers
# a dust that climbs into the low dust, which lights the output and, changing power beside the
# piston, updates it, so the piston fires too and the pulse is the usual 5 gt. After a placement
# the low dust is already lit by the open step when the observer fires, so nothing new reaches the
# piston during its push. The dust west of the low dust keeps it from pointing into the piston.
origin: -2 -2 -1
port target: 0 -1 0
port output: -2 0 1
