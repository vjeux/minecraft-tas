# CIRCUIT: bud-latched-qc-fast bud detects place remove state update, latency 1 gt, reset manual, off 3 gt after it, re-arm 2 gt, footprint 5 cells, 7 blocks, 3x3x3, robust
name: bud-latched-qc-fast
circuit: bud
# A latched BUD (manual reset) with a 1 gt latency: the sticky piston, armed by quasi-connectivity
# from the redstone block above the stone in front of it, pushes that stone off the step between
# two dusts in the tick after the update; the low dust joins the high one (powered by its redstone
# block) and lights the output, and the piston stays out, so the output holds. The reset input is
# the arming redstone block: taking it away updates the piston through its head, it pulls the stone
# back onto the step (the output goes off 3 gt after the reset starts); putting it back arms the
# piston without updating it.
origin: -1 -2 -1
port target: -1 0 0
port output: 1 -1 -1
port input.reset: 1 1 0 minecraft:air minecraft:redstone_block

  X minecraft:stone
  R minecraft:redstone_block
