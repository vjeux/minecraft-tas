# CIRCUIT: bud-slime-qc-fast bud detects place remove state update, latency 1 gt, pulse 5 gt, re-arm 6 gt, jams update pair 2 gt apart, footprint 4 cells, 9 blocks, 2x5x3, robust
name: bud-slime-qc-fast
circuit: bud
# The slime-block QC BUD with a 1 gt latency: a stone block stuck to the slime block sits on the
# step between two dusts and cuts it. A block update reaching the piston (armed by the redstone
# block on the slime block, through quasi-connectivity) starts the push in the very next tick: the
# stone leaves the step at once (a moving block is no conductor), the low dust joins the high dust
# (powered by its redstone block) and lights the output 1 gt after the update, until the stone comes
# back down with the retracting slime block. The high dust points away from the stone, so the stone
# is no power source of its own. A shape-only change sends no block update: not detected.
origin: -1 -1 -1
port target: -1 0 0
port output: -1 0 1
