name: cal-dust-encoder2
circuit: encoder 2
# A plain 4-to-2 encoder of bare dust: on one-hot inputs, q0 = d1 OR d3 and q1 = d2 OR d3, so two
# dust lines suffice. d3's cell sits between the two lines (it powers both without joining them:
# the lines are two cells apart), d1 touches only the q0 line and d2 only the q1 line; d0 (index
# 0) drives nothing. Instant (0 gt). Two inputs at once are outside a plain encoder's domain: d1
# and d2 together give q = 11, which a priority encoder would have to read as 10.
origin: 0 0 0
port input.d0: 6 1 2
port input.d1: 0 1 0
port input.d2: 0 1 4
port input.d3: 2 1 2
port output.q0: 4 1 1
port output.q1: 4 1 3
