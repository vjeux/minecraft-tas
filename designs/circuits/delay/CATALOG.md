The input also reaches the sticky piston beside E by quasi-connectivity (the input touches the cell above the piston; E's power change updates the piston), which pushes the block east next to the output: 1 gt after the rise the block leaves D, which straightens into the output AND drops from 15 to 14 (a power change: the output cell gets the block update); 3 gt after the rise the block lands next to the output and holds it on for the gt after the fall, until the piston pulls it back 1 gt after the fall. |
