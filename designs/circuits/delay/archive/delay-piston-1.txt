# CIRCUIT: delay-piston-1 delay N 1 gt, min-pulse 3 gt, min-gap 3 gt, 4 blocks, 3x2x2, robust
# Delay line N = 1 gt (track delay): a dust between the input (west) and the output cell (east),
# bent at rest by a redstone block south of it (which powers it but makes it point north-south).
# The input powers a sticky piston that pushes that block east, next to the output cell: the block
# leaves the dust 1 gt after the rise (the dust, powered by the input, straightens and points into
# the output) and arrives next to the output 3 gt after it; after the fall the block keeps the
# output on until the piston pulls it back, 1 gt after the fall.
