| [seven-segment-analog](archive/seven-segment-analog.txt) | seven-segment 4 | latency 34 gt (a0 falls from a=1111), glitches 65 | 566 | 7x16x7 | robust | A 7-segment display decoder: four input lines (a 4-bit number) light the seven segments of its hex digit, 0-9 then A b C d E F, in a 7x7 tower 16 high. A chain of subtract comparators on top turns the four bits into one signal strength s = 15 - (8 a0' + 2 a1 + 4 a2 + a3); s flows down the tower losslessly through comparator bridges. Seven rings stacked every 2 levels, one per segment: each lamp is lit by window comparators (a hopper behind each sets its window, its sides read s and a copy of its complement), so each segment lights exactly for its set of strengths. Catalog parts: comparator gates (subtract) and constant hoppers. |
