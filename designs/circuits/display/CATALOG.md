| [seven-segment-stack-v4](archive/seven-segment-stack-v4.txt) | seven-segment 4 | latency 54 gt (a0 rises from a=1100), glitches 65 | 627 | 9x18x9 | robust | A 7-segment display decoder: four input lines (a 4-bit number) light the seven segments of its hex digit, 0-9 then A b C d E F, in a 9x9 tower 18 high.
