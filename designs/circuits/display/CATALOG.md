| [seven-segment-therm-v2](archive/seven-segment-therm-v2.txt) | seven-segment 4 | latency 178 gt (a0 rises from a=0000) | 1989 | 41x8x80 | robust | A 7-segment display: four input lines (a 4-bit number) light the seven lamps of a readable digit on its front face, 0-9 then A b C d E F, with no flicker while the number changes (glitch-free). A chain of subtract comparators turns the four bits into one signal strength; a dust ruler from it gives every threshold; each run of values a segment lights is one subtract comparator between two thresholds (a repeater tap behind, the next tap at its side); repeater lanes carry each segment to its lamp, with the inputs' delays equalised so every change arrives in the same tick.
