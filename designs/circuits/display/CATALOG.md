| [seven-segment-rings7](archive/seven-segment-rings7.txt) | seven-segment 4 | latency 74 gt (a0 falls from a=0011), glitches 2 | 643 | 22x7x19 | robust | A hex 7-segment display: set the four input levers to a value 0-15 and the seven lamps on the front face show its digit (0-9, then A b C d E F). Inside, the four bits become one signal strength (a chain of four subtract comparators reading composters; the faster inputs' repeaters are slowed so every bit arrives in the same tick), carried over comparator lines to seven rings of comparators, one per segment, stacked in three towers behind and beside the face. Each ring's comparators each pass one range of strengths (a hopper sets the range; a second comparator gives the lower edge), the ring's centre dust is lit when any of them passes, and a repeater lane carries it to the segment's lamp; each lane starts with a 3-tick repeater, which stays on through the rings' short hand-over dips. The bottom segment's ring computes the segment's complement; a torch in its lane inverts it back between two such repeaters, so its short pulses go too. Only the top ring of each tower has its own line: the rings below read the strength through the blocks the ring above powers right over them, and each ring keeps only the dust and bridges its comparators need. |
