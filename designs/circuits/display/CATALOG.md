Inside, the four bits become one signal strength (a chain of four subtract comparators reading composters), which climbs to seven rings of comparators, one per segment, stacked in three towers behind and beside the face. Each ring's comparators each pass one range of strengths (a hopper sets the range; a second comparator gives the lower edge), the ring's centre dust is lit when any of them passes, and a repeater lane carries it to the segment's lamp.
