# CIRCUIT: lift-chain-n0 item-elevator 4 items/s 2.50, first item 30 gt, at rest no, bill 5 iron, 7 quartz, 19 redstone, 13 blocks, 4x5x1, robust
name: lift-chain-n0
# A dropper elevator 4 blocks up (13 blocks): the hopper under the input chest feeds a column of
# droppers facing up, the top one pushing into the output chest. One observer clock (6 gt, its partner
# placed last: a player starts it) fires the column through a chain of observers facing down; the
# observer whose back touches a dropper powers it hard, and a hard-powered dropper powers the ones
# above and below it. The clock's own observer fires droppers 0-2, a four-observer bend fires 3-5 4 gt
# later, then every two blocks of a straight chain fire the next three 4 gt after the ones below:
# a dropper also reads the power of the dropper two above (quasi-connectivity), so each group must be
# off whenever the group below turns off.
circuit: item-elevator 4
extend family: dropper-chain
extend axis: 4 + 2n
extend blocks: 13 + 5n
extend rule: two more droppers on the column, two more chain observers and one observer firing the new top group
extend n: 0..8
extend base: 0
extend generator: mctas circuit gen dropper-lift --n {n} --out {out}

port start: 3 1 0 last
