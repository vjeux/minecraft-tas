# CIRCUIT: lift20-chain-53b item-elevator 20 items/s 2.50, first item 110 gt, at rest no, bill 5 iron, 31 quartz, 83 redstone, 53 blocks, 4x21x1, robust
name: lift20-chain-53b
# A dropper elevator twenty blocks up: the hopper under the input chest feeds a column of 21 droppers
# facing up, the top one pushing into the output chest. One observer clock (6 gt, its partner placed
# last as a player starts it) fires the column through a chain of observers facing down: the observer
# whose back touches a dropper powers it hard, and a hard-powered dropper powers the ones above and
# below it. The clock's own observer fires droppers 0-2; a four-observer bend carries the pulse to the
# observer firing droppers 3-5 4 gt later; then a straight chain climbs a block every 2 gt and an
# observer every two blocks fires the next three droppers 4 gt after the ones below. A dropper also
# reads the power of the dropper two above (quasi-connectivity), so each group must be off whenever
# the group below turns off: the 4 gt offsets keep it so. Same family as lift5-chain-14b and
# lift10-chain-28b: each two more blocks of height add two droppers, two chain observers and one.
