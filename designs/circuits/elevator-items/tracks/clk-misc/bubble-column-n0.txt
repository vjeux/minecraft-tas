# A bubble-column elevator 4 blocks up (40 blocks): the hopper under the input chest feeds a dropper
# that shoots each item into a water column over soul sand; the bubble column lifts it to the top
# source, under a ceiling, whose flow toward the flowing water beside it carries it back over the
# collecting hopper, on the dropper's side (the items drift away from the dropper as they rise, and the
# hollow under a hopper's rim beside the column would catch one), which pushes it into the output chest.
# Two observers facing each other in the column's wall are the clock (6 gt): the lower one's back powers
# the stone above the dropper hard (a hard-powered dropper would lock the hopper beside it); the upper
# one is placed last (a player starts the clock by placing it). No redstone part grows with the height:
# the bill stays 10 iron, 2 quartz, 5 redstone (from 4 blocks up: lower, the upper observer's back would
# lock the collecting hopper).
