name: bubble10-rest-75b
# The bubble-rest family's member n = 7, archived as a design of its own (`mctas circuit gen bubble-rest --n 7 --standalone bubble10-rest-75b`).
# A bubble-column elevator 10 blocks up (75 blocks) that rests when no item comes: the hopper under the
# input chest feeds a dropper that shoots each item into a water column over soul sand; the bubble
# column lifts it to the top source, under a ceiling, whose flow carries it back over the collecting
# hopper on the dropper's side (the items drift away from the dropper as they rise), which pushes it
# into the output chest. The dropper's clock runs only while the dropper holds an item: a comparator
# reads the dropper, and its signal reaches the sticky piston below the cell in front of it (a piston
# is also powered through the cell above it), which pushes an observer up beside the clock's other
# observer; the pair then pulses every 6 gt, and the lower one's back powers the stone above the
# dropper hard (the hopper beside the dropper stays unpowered). With the dropper empty the piston pulls
# the observer back down and the clock stops.
