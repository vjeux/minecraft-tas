name: bubble5-rest
# A bubble-column elevator five blocks up that rests when no item comes: the hopper under the input
# chest feeds a dropper that shoots each item into a water column over soul sand; the bubble column
# lifts it to the top source, under a ceiling, whose flow carries it over the collecting hopper,
# which pushes it into the output chest. The dropper's clock runs only while the dropper holds an
# item: a comparator reads the dropper, and its signal reaches the sticky piston below the cell in
# front of it (a piston is also powered through the cell above it), which pushes an observer up
# beside the clock's other observer; the pair then pulses every 6 gt, and the lower one's back powers
# the stone above the dropper hard (the hopper beside the dropper stays unpowered). When the dropper is
# empty the piston pulls the observer back down and the clock stops.
