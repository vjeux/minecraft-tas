name: cactus-wool-9b
# A piston beside the cactus's second cell, a sculk sensor beside the piston, a wool block above that
# cell and a stone on each of its two other sides. The piston and the stones are solid, so a cactus
# block that grows into that cell breaks itself a tick later; the sensor hears the block break (a
# vibration) and powers the piston for 30 ticks: the head sweeps the cell and shoves the cactus item (a
# cactus item touching a cactus dies in 5 ticks; the stones and the wool keep it inside the head's
# sweep) into the cell beyond, where a hopper minecart standing on a hopper takes it at once; the hopper
# empties it into the chest. Items landing on wool make no vibration, so things falling onto the farm
# do not keep the sensor busy. A stone holds up the sand. Copies stand 9 blocks apart (a sensor hears
# 8 blocks around it).
