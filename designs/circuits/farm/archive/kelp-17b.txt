name: kelp-17b
# Kelp planted on a hopper. An observer watches the bottom kelp, which turns from kelp to kelp_plant
# when the kelp grows a block: its pulse powers the stone behind it, whose dust climbs onto a wall to
# the piston above the second block. The piston pushes down into the second block: it breaks it and
# pushes the item down into the bottom block's cell, where the hopper below takes it (kelp has no
# collision, and the hopper reaches through it). A water source beside the second block refills it;
# an item the piston head squeezes out into that water floats up to its surface, where a second hopper
# under the source takes it. Copies stand side by side, 4 blocks apart.
