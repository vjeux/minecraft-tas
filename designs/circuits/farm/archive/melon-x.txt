name: melon-x
# A melon stem on wet farmland with a fruit cell on each of its four sides (on mud) and wet farmland
# on the four diagonals; a waterlogged grate beside one fruit cell keeps the farmland wet. Wet
# farmland around makes the stem roll 1 in 4 instead of 1 in 13 (CropBlock.getGrowthSpeed), and with
# all four sides free every roll grows a melon. The observer above the stem fires when it attaches
# to a melon; the dust on its top powers the cells above the four pistons, which push down into the
# fruit cells and break the melon. The squeezed slices go down through the mud into a hopper
# minecart under it, which a hopper empties into a chest.
