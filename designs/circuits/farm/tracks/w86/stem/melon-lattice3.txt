# A full-size melon farm: 3 x 3 stems two blocks apart on wet farmland, wet farmland on every
# diagonal and a fruit cell (on mud) on every side of every stem, each shared by the two stems
# beside it. Wet farmland around makes a stem roll 1 in 4 (CropBlock.getGrowthSpeed), and with four
# free sides every roll grows a melon. An observer above each stem fires when it attaches; the dust on
# its top powers the cells above the four pistons around it, which push down into the fruit cells.
# The squeezed drop goes down through the mud into a hopper minecart, which a hopper empties into a
# chest. Waterlogged grates on diagonals keep the farmland wet. Written by `circuit farm-gen`.
