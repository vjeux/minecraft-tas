# A full-size melon farm: 8 x 8 stems two blocks apart on wet farmland, wet farmland on every
# diagonal and a fruit cell (on mud) on every side of every stem but the rim's, each shared by the two
# stems beside it (a fruit that grows in an observer's 2 gt blind spot is broken by the other stem's

# its top powers the cells above the four pistons around it, which push down into the fruit cells.
# The squeezed drop goes down through the mud into a hopper minecart, which a hopper empties into a
# chest. Waterlogged grates on diagonals keep the farmland wet. Written by `circuit farm-gen`.
