name: melon-row3
# Three melon stems in a row, two blocks apart, on wet farmland, with wet farmland on every diagonal
# and a fruit cell (on mud) on every side: the cells between two stems are shared, which costs no
# melons since a cell holds a melon only for the few ticks before its piston breaks it. Wet farmland
# around makes each stem roll 1 in 4 instead of 1 in 13 (CropBlock.getGrowthSpeed), and with all
# four sides free every roll grows a melon. The observer above a stem fires when it attaches to a
# melon; the dust on its top powers the cells above the pistons around it, which push down into the
# fruit cells and break the melon. The piston squeezes the slices into the mud, which lets them out
# through its nearest open face: stones close the outer faces, so they go down into the hopper
# minecart under it, which a hopper empties into a chest; a hopper under each stem's farmland takes
# a drop that lands beside the stem. A waterlogged grate keeps it all wet.
