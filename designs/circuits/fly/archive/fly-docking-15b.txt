# CIRCUIT: fly-docking-15b flying-machine two-way round trip 98 gt, switch docks within 87 gt, restarts within 0 gt, 15 blocks, 5x2x9, robust
# Flying machine with docking stations, 15 blocks, and an on/off lever: a 6-block flying machine (two sticky pistons, two slime blocks, two observers) shuttles between two docking stations, 98 ticks per round trip. Each station has an obsidian wall that stops the machine and an observer that sees it arrive and fires, 2 ticks later, into a dust behind the leading piston, which pushes the machine back the other way. At the station with the lever, the observer stands on a sticky piston: lever off pulls it aside, so the machine docks there and stays; lever on pushes it back into place, and a moved observer fires, so the docked machine leaves at once and shuttles again.
