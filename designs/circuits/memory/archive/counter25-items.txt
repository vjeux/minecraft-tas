# CIRCUIT: counter25-items counter 25 N 25, output toggles every 25 pulses, 39 blocks, 5x3x6, robust
# Item counter, 25 items, 39 blocks (track memory, `mctas circuit memory items --n 25`): the one counter whose size does not depend on N. Droppers A and B face each other and hold 25 items between them; each pulse fires exactly one of them, so the pile walks from A to B and back and each dropper runs empty once every 25 pulses. Comparators a and b read them (level 0 only when empty), so the wall torches on their output blocks are lit exactly when their own dropper is empty, and both torches power the copper bulb W above them: W toggles once every 25 pulses and is the output. W is also the phase. Comparator C reads it and its dust line powers the stone south of A, which holds A; the wall torch on that stone is lit only when the line is off, and its line holds B through the stone east of it. A held dropper is one kept continuously powered: it sees no rising edge, so a pulse fires only the other one. The pulse is the dust on the two lids, since a dropper fires on a signal at its own cell or at the cell above it.
