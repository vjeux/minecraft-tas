# CIRCUIT: dff-lock dff latency 6 gt, edge rising, setup 1 gt, hold 1 gt, 18 blocks, 3x3x4, robust
# D flip-flop, repeater-lock master-slave: the data runs through two repeaters A then B; each has a lock repeater beside it, and a repeater that is locked holds its output. The master lock reads the clock straight, so A follows d only while the clock is LOW; the slave lock reads the clock through a wall torch, so B passes only while the clock is HIGH. On the rising edge A freezes on d and B opens, so q takes d exactly at the edge.
