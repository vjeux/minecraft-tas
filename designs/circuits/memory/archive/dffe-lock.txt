# CIRCUIT: dffe-lock dff-enable latency 6 gt, edge rising, 18 blocks, 2x3x6, robust
# D flip-flop with enable, 18 blocks: the 15-block repeater-lock flip-flop plus one more lock on the slave. The data runs through two repeaters; the master's lock reads the clock straight (it follows d while the clock is low) and the slave's lock reads it through a wall torch (it passes while the clock is high). The enable gets its own wall torch and repeater, locking the slave from its other side whenever e is off, so a clock edge with e off leaves q alone. Gating the slave instead of the clock needs no AND gate: a repeater is locked if EITHER side is powered.
name: dffe-lock
circuit: dff-enable

port input.d: -1 1 0
port input.e: 1 1 -3
port output: 2 1 0
