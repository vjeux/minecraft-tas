# CIRCUIT: tffr-dropring tff-reset latency 13 gt, reset 6 gt, min-pulse 1 gt, 8 blocks, 3x3x2, robust
# T flip-flop with reset, 8 blocks (track memory): the dropper-ring T flip-flop (two droppers, two hoppers between them, a comparator reading dropper B) with a reset input next to dropper B only. A t pulse fires both droppers (A directly, B through the dust line), only the one holding the stone moves it, and the hoppers hand it to the other dropper after both have fired: q toggles. An r pulse fires B alone: a stone in B leaves (q off at once) and lands in A; with the stone in A nothing moves.
