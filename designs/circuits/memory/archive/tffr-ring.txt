# CIRCUIT: tffr-ring tff-reset latency 13 gt, reset 6 gt, min-pulse 2 gt (the shortest a real input makes), 7 blocks, 3x3x2, robust
# T flip-flop with reset, 7 blocks: two droppers and two hoppers in a ring pass one stone around, and a comparator reading the second dropper is the output. A t pulse fires both droppers; only the one holding the stone moves it, into a hopper that hands it on 8 ticks later, after both droppers have fired, so q toggles. One dust on the first hopper, under the t input block, fires both droppers at once: it points into the cells above them, and a dropper also fires on power in the cell above it. An r pulse fires the second dropper alone: if it holds the stone, the stone leaves and q clears; otherwise nothing moves.
name: tffr-ring

port input.r: 1 0 2
