# D flip-flop as two D latches in series with opposite enables: the master follows d while the clock is low, the slave copies the master while the clock is high, so q changes only on the clock's rising edge. Each latch is the archived 5-block one (a repeater carrying d, locked by a subtract comparator that reads a redstone block at its back and the enable at its side). The clock's own input block carries a torch, which is the inverted enable for the master, and powers a dust that is the slave's enable.
