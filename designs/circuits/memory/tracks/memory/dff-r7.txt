# D flip-flop from two D latches in series with opposite enables: the master follows d while the clock is low, the slave copies the master while the clock is high, so q takes d exactly on the clock's rising edge. Each latch is the archived 8-block one (a repeater carrying the data, locked by a subtract comparator with a redstone block at its back and the enable at its side). The clock arrives on the design's own repeater K and splits: straight into the slave's comparator side, and through a torch into the master's, so the two enables are opposite and never share a side cell.
