| [pext-n3-cascade-v1](archive/pext-n3-cascade-v1.txt) | piston-extender 3 | N 3, extend 38 gt, retract 42 gt | 26 | 9x4x4 | robust | N=3 CASCADE extender (wired): units [slime + sticky piston + observer under the slime] in a line (A, unit B, unit C, payload). A unit that is moved fires its own piston 2 gt after landing, so the pulse-toggle train C B C A B C needs only the three round starts from outside, all from the lever: C at t4 (pulsers above-beside x5 and x7, quasi-connectivity), B at t12 (x3) then t14 (x4), A at t26 (observer on A's top). Pulsers sit diagonal to the slime cells so no slime ever drags them. |
