# CIRCUIT: pext-n3-flat-cascade16 piston-extender 3 N 3, extend 28 gt, retract 34 gt, 16 blocks, 9x6x1, robust
# Pushes a block out three cells from a one-block-wide wall, 16 blocks: a line of three sticky
# pistons with a slime block between each pair; each piston fires itself whenever it is moved (an
# observer rides on the slime behind it), and the observers above the line, run by a chain from the
# lever, start each wave of pushes (out) or grabs (back).
# How it works: going out, the middle piston pushes the front piston and the block, the front piston
# pushes the block on, then the back piston starts a second wave (back, middle, front); coming back the
# waves are front; middle, front; back, middle, front, each piston grabbing what the one in front left
# behind. The back and middle waves start with observers two above the line that power their piston
# through the cell above it. The front piston's observer rides ON TOP of its slime, facing up: when the
# lever goes off, the sticky piston under the lever pulls back right above it, the riding observer sees
# it and fires, and the front piston starts the way back with no starter of its own. The lever hangs on
# the side of the chain's first observer. Found by `mctas circuit pext lns --free-lever` (remove 3
# blocks, the lever included, grow 2 back: 19 -> 18 -> 17 -> 16); game: robust (16 builds), redsim
# agrees.
