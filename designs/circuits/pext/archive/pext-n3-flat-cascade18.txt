# CIRCUIT: pext-n3-flat-cascade18 piston-extender 3 N 3, extend 29 gt, retract 35 gt, 18 blocks, 9x6x1, robust
# Pushes a block out three cells from a one-block-wide wall, 18 blocks: a line of three sticky
# pistons with a slime block between each pair; each piston fires itself whenever it is moved (an
# observer hangs under the slime behind it), and the observers above the line, run by a chain from the
# lever, start each wave of pushes (out) or grabs (back).
# How it works: going out, the middle piston pushes the front piston and the block, the front piston
# pushes the block on, then the back piston starts a second wave (back, middle, front); coming back the
# waves are front; middle, front; back, middle, front, each piston grabbing what the one in front left
# behind. Every wave starts with an observer two above the line that powers its piston through the
# empty cell above it; the lever's chain is sped up by two pistons whose heads the next observer sees,
# and a sticky piston standing above the middle piston's rest cell replaces one starter and two chain
# observers. Found by `mctas circuit pext lns` from the 19-block cascade (remove 3 blocks, grow 2
# back); game: robust (16 builds), redsim agrees.
