# CIRCUIT: pext-n3-rider20-7x4x3 piston-extender 3 N 3, extend 15 gt, retract 18 gt, 20 blocks, 7x4x3, robust
# A triple piston extender, the fastest so far (20 blocks, 7x4x3): the payload goes out 3 cells in
# 15 gt and comes back in 18 gt. The back sticky piston (lever on its back) pushes a row of five
# slime blocks; the two other sticky pistons and the payload sit on the row and ride along, with
# two observers stuck to its side. As the row lands, those observers, a sticky piston that lifts
# and drops a redstone block, and a short observer line over the top fire the middle and the front
# piston in turn, out and back. When it is built, one top observer fires and the top piston
# pushes it one cell north: that is its resting place. Found by `mctas circuit pext anneal` from
# pext-n3-rider24 (pext).
