# CIRCUIT: pext-n5-flat-cascade31 piston-extender 5 N 5, extend 48 gt, retract 64 gt, 31 blocks, 15x6x1, robust
# Pushes a block out five cells from a one-block-wide wall, 31 blocks: a line of five sticky pistons
# with a slime block between each pair; each piston fires itself whenever it is moved (an observer
# hangs under the slime behind it), and observers above the line, run by a chain from the lever, start
# each wave of pushes (out) or grabs (back).
# How it works: the cascade train (going out the pistons push in waves; coming back the waves start
# front first and each piston grabs what the one in front left behind), every wave started by an
# observer two above the line that powers its piston through the empty cell above it; the lever hangs
# on the side of the chain's first observer and repeaters in the chain stretch the waits between
# waves. Found by `mctas circuit pext lns --free-lever` from the 33-block cascade (33 -> 31, faster
# too); game: robust (16 builds), redsim agrees.
