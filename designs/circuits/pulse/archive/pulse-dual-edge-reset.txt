# Extendable family dual-edge-reset (n = 0): dual-edge with L = 30 + 16n gt, 32 + 4n blocks. an observer watching the input fires on both edges through a repeater into a copper bulb (locked by a comparator reading the bulb while it is lit); the comparator also feeds a U of delay-4 repeaters into a second bulb, read by a comparator that an observer watches: its back faces the first bulb, so each loop sends one 2 gt reset pulse that turns the bulb off.
