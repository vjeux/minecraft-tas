# CIRCUIT: pulse-fall-odd-L3 falling-edge L 3 gt, delay 7 gt, min-pulse 6 gt, 16 blocks, 5x2x4, robust
name: pulse-fall-odd-L3
circuit: falling-edge
# Odd falling-edge pulses: two falling-edge detectors 1 gt apart: a sticky piston pulls back an
# observer that fires 2 gt after it lands (5 gt after the edge), and a redstone torch the input
# turns off (through dust into its block) lights again 2 gt after the edge and toggles a copper
# bulb, read by a comparator an observer watches (6 gt after the edge); each feeds a delay-1
# repeater (2 gt pulses) into one dust, where the two pulses overlap into 2 + 1 = 3 gt.
origin: -2 0 -1
port input: 0 1 -1
port output: 1 1 2
