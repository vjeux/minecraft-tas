# CIRCUIT: pulse-fall-psub-L15 falling-edge L 15 gt, delay 5 gt, min-pulse 15 gt, 14 blocks, 6x2x2, robust
name: pulse-fall-psub-L15
circuit: falling-edge
group: pulse-fall-psub
group title: odd-length falling-edge pulse: a piston-moved redstone block
# Odd falling-edge pulses (L = 15 gt): a sticky piston the input powers pushes a redstone block out from behind a subtract comparator and pulls it back on the falling edge (the piston moves the tick after the input, the block lands in the block-entity phase: an odd delay); the comparator's side gets the input inverted by a redstone torch (through dust into its block) and delayed by two repeaters (4 and 4: an even delay), so after the falling edge the output is on from the block's return until the torch's signal arrives: L = 2 x (4 + 4) - 1 = 15 gt.
origin: -2 0 -1
port input: 0 1 -1
port output: 2 1 1
