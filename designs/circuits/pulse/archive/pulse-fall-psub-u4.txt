name: pulse-fall-psub-u4
# Extendable family fall-psub-u4 (n = 0): falling-edge with L = 15 + 16n gt, 22 + 4n blocks. a sticky piston the input powers pushes a redstone block out from behind a subtract comparator and pulls it back on the falling edge (an odd delay: the block lands in the block-entity phase); the comparator's side gets the input inverted by a redstone torch and delayed by a U of delay-4 repeaters (an even delay), so after the falling edge the output is on for an odd number of ticks. Extension: lengthen the repeater U by one row: one more delay-4 repeater (on stone) in each column. Generator: mctas circuit pulse family fall-psub-u4 --n N --out FILE.
