# CIRCUIT: pulse-limiter-odd-L3 pulse-limiter L 3 gt, delay 6 gt, min-pulse 4 gt, 20 blocks, 4x2x5, robust
name: pulse-limiter-odd-L3
circuit: pulse-limiter
# Odd pulse limiter: every input pulse becomes one pulse of an odd length: two rising-edge detectors 1 gt apart: a copper bulb the input toggles
# on every rising edge, read by a comparator an observer watches (fires 4 gt after the edge), and
# a sticky piston pushing an observer that fires 2 gt after it lands (5 gt after the edge; pulled
# back on the falling edge it fires into an empty cell); each feeds a delay-1 repeater (2 gt
# pulses) into one dust line, where the two pulses overlap into 2 + 1 = 3 gt.
origin: -1 0 -1
port input: 0 1 -1
port output: 0 1 3
