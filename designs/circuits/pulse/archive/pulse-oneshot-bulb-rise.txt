# Extendable family oneshot-bulb-rise (n = 0): rising-edge with L = 18 + 16n gt, 19 + 4n blocks. the input toggles a copper bulb on every rising edge (even 1 gt), a comparator reads it and an observer watching that comparator fires once per rising edge into a second bulb; a comparator reads the second bulb into a U of delay-4 repeaters back into it, which turns it off one loop later. Extension: lengthen the repeater U by one row: one more delay-4 repeater (on stone) in each column. Generator: mctas circuit pulse family oneshot-bulb-rise --n N --out FILE.
