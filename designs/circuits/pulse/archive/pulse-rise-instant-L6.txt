# The fastest rising-edge detector: its pulse starts in the very tick the input turns on (delay 0),
# 6 ticks long, 8 blocks. The input lights the output at once through two dusts and a stone beside
# the output; a repeater passes the input to a sticky piston 6 ticks later, which pushes that stone
# away and cuts the output; when the input turns off, the piston pulls the stone back. Detail:
# a pushed block conducts nothing from the moment it moves; the repeater's delay sets the length.
