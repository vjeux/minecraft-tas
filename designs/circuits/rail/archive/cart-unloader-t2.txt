# CIRCUIT: cart-unloader-t2 cart-unloader line terminus, unload rate 2.47 items/s, departure delay 15 gt, 25 blocks, 5x3x3, robust
# cart-unloader terminus v2 (clk-obs, 2026-10-21): first working layout, not minimal.
# The cart rolls in from the west over a booster (an always-on powered rail: even a crawling cart
# reaches the end fast), a detector rail and a plain rail onto a powered rail E over a hopper H, and
# stops against the end block. H pulls its items into the judge's chest below.
# E is powered (through a torch inverter, a dust line, a repeater into the end block) only when no
# item is in H and the detector rail has been quiet for its 20 gt: so an arriving cart parks (the
# detector holds E off until H has its first item), a loaded cart waits while H keeps getting items,
# and an empty cart (or one just emptied) is kicked away from the end block, back west.
