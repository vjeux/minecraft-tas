| [rand2-token](archive/rand2-token.txt) | randomizer 2 | style hold, latency 8 gt, presses 48 | 19 | 10x3x1 | robust | Press the button and one of the two outputs turns on at random, each half the time, and stays on until the next press picks again. Inside, two droppers face each other: one holds an iron shovel (the token), the other a cobblestone. A press fires both in the same tick; whichever fires first hands its item over, and the other then fires one of its two items back at random, so the shovel ends in either dropper with chance 1/2 whatever came before. A comparator reads each dropper (2 with the shovel, 1 with the cobblestone), two dusts bring that down to 1 or 0, and a repeater lights the output while the shovel is there. |
