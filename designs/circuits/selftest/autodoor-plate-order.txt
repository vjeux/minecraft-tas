# nobody inside).

# next to the near piston's frame, and when it turns off it updates that piston (see lock-v3 for the
# frame without a piston head under it that held the piston on). One dust and its support fewer
# under the inner plates.
# lock-v3: the door is classic6 (four sticky pistons, the frame on top, dust on the frame): the dust's
# source is the top torch of a 3-torch tower beside the door, so the door is powered (closed) exactly

# inner plates (z = +-1); the outer plates (z = +-3) unlock it through the lock torch (one per door
# now): see lock-v1 for why the door stays open for someone standing in the doorway.
# The far outer row's dust drops under the floor (y = -4) and climbs into the near row's path,
# which points into the lock torch's base.
