## load 1-stack

| design | kind | metrics | blocks | box | robustness | how it works |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [shulker-ref-loader-1](archive/shulker-ref-loader-1.txt) | shulker-loader 1 | boxes/min 5.357, items/s 2.41, first box 239 gt, cycle 224 gt | 29 | 6x4x5 | robust | Reference shulker loader (track shulker, hand-built). The dispenser sits ABOVE the box cell X (so the drop, which pops at a random offset and rises, can never leave X upward); a hopper feeds X from the side. A comparator reads X through a stone, in compare mode with a redstone block on its side, so it outputs 15 only when X is completely full; through a stone, two dusts and a repeater it powers a stone beside a sticky piston, which pushes a stone into X: the box breaks and drops with its contents, and the moving stone pushes the drop into the walled drop cell, over the hopper that sends it to the output chest. The piston is two cells from X so that the powered dispenser never powers it (quasi-connectivity). An observer watching the pushed stone's cell fires, through a stone, a dust on the dispenser, which places the next box from its hopper. A start lever, watched by a second observer and delayed 8 gt by a repeater (the first box needs 9 gt to reach the dispenser), places the first box. |

## unload 1-stack

| design | kind | metrics | blocks | box | robustness | how it works |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [shulker-ref-unloader-1](archive/shulker-ref-unloader-1.txt) | shulker-unloader 1 | boxes/min 5.455, items/s 2.45, first box 238 gt, cycle 220 gt | 47 | 6x5x8 | robust | Reference shulker unloader (track shulker, hand-built). The dispenser above the box cell X places a full box; the hopper under X empties it into the items chest. A comparator reads X through a stone; a repeater squares it (15 while the box holds anything) onto a dust that feeds a subtract comparator twice: directly on its side, and 4 gt late on its rear. When the box runs empty, the side drops first, so the subtract comparator gives a 4 gt pulse (never on a new full box: then the side rises first). The pulse fires a sticky piston that pushes a stone into X: the empty box breaks and drops, and the moving stone pushes the drop into the walled drop cell, over the hopper that sends it to the empties chest. The same pulse, 2 gt later through a repeater, locks the unloading hopper so it cannot pick the drop up. An observer watching the pushed stone's cell fires, through a stone, a dust on the dispenser for the next box. A start lever, watched by a second observer and delayed 8 gt by a repeater, places the first box. |
