# Always-on shulker unloader (track shulker, hand-built): the reference unloader with its start
# lever replaced by a comparator that reads the dispenser through the drop cell's roof. An observer
# above that comparator fires the dispenser whenever it goes from holding no box to holding one: it
# places the first full box by itself, and again whenever full boxes come back after the supply
# ran out (badges always-on and box-starved). The rest is the reference unloader: the dispenser
# above the box cell, the unloading hopper under it, a squared comparator reading feeding a
# subtract comparator directly and 4 gt late (a pulse when the box runs empty, never on a new full
# box), a sticky piston two cells away pushing a stone into the box, the moving stone pushing the
# empty box's drop into a walled cell over the empties hopper, the pulse locking the unloading
# hopper 2 gt later so it cannot take the drop, and an observer on the pushed stone's cell firing
# the dispenser after each box.
