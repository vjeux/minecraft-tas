# A one-block-wide shulker loader you can line up side by side with no gap (41 blocks, 1x6x11): a
# dispenser under the box spot places an empty box, the hopper above fills it from the items chest,
# and when the box is full a piston behind it breaks it; the dropped full box lands on the hopper
# under the box's front, which sends it to the output chest.
# How it works: the full test and the copper bulb of the 39-block loader (the bulb toggles once per
# full box; an observer on the comparator that reads it starts the line of observers over the top
# to the breaking piston). The bottom row is gone: the next box is fired from the boxes side. A
# comparator reads the first hopper of the box line (a box waits there 8 gt, so it sees boxes
# arrive even one at a time, or from a chest kept full); its pulse and the pulse of an observer
# watching the observer above the breaking piston both power a dropper; the dropper fires a
# piston, an observer sees its head, and observers and lamps pass it down to an observer that
# fires the box dispenser through a stone under it. It passes the judge's full-chest,
# endless-supply and one-box checks. Hand-built from the 39-block loader (pext).
