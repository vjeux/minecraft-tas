# A one-block-wide shulker loader you can line up side by side with no gap (28 blocks, 1x7x8): a
# dispenser under the box spot places an empty box, the hopper above fills it from the items chest,
# and when the box is full a piston behind it breaks it; the dropped full box lands on the hopper
# under the box's front, which sends it to the output chest.
# How it works: the full test of the 39-block loader (a comparator on the box, a block, the redstone
# line shared with the neighbouring copies over a lamp, and a comparator reading that lamp with an
# empty dispenser behind it, which passes only a full 15). An observer pulses a sticky piston on
# every change of the test; a sticky piston given a short pulse leaves the block it pushed behind,
# so it lifts an observer one cell when the box becomes full and pulls it back down on the next
# change. Up there the observer fires a piston, another observer and a sticky piston over the top,
# and the observer above the breaking piston sees that last piston head: the box breaks once per
# box, with no copper bulb. Down there the lifted observer fires into air. Along the bottom, a
# piston under the test lamp and an observer fire a dispenser on every change of the test, and the
# observer watching that dispenser fires the box dispenser: the second time, the broken box is gone
# and the next one goes in. Found by `mctas circuit shulker anneal --soft-volume` (random edits
# judged on redsim; the box may grow and shrink) from the 33-block loader.
