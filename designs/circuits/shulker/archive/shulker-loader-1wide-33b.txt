# A one-block-wide shulker loader you can line up side by side with no gap (33 blocks): a dispenser
# under the box spot places an empty box, the hopper above fills it from the items chest, and when
# the box is full a piston behind it breaks it; the dropped full box lands on the hopper under the
# box's front, which sends it to the output chest.
# How it works: the full test of the 39-block loader (a comparator on the box, a second comparator,
# the redstone line shared with the neighbouring copies over a stone, and a comparator reading that
# stone that passes only a full 15), a copper bulb toggled by each full box, and signals carried by
# pistons and observers: a piston head that moves one cell is seen by the next observer (two cells
# per piston), and a dispenser serves as the block the observer under the box dispenser watches.
# Found by `mctas circuit shulker anneal` (random edits, each judged on redsim with the judge's own
# scenarios) from the 39-block loader: same box, same badges, same speed.
