# A shulker box loader whose copies stand side by side every 3 blocks, judged on a real box read
# (1 wide is impossible: a box cell in a 1-wide slice has 4 free faces for 5 jobs, and every
# signal there is on/off, so "full" (15 against 14) cannot be read; 2 wide cannot carry a signal
# from one column to the other without touching the neighbour's).
#
# How it works:
# - The box cell X has the feed hopper above it, the dispenser below it (facing up), the box read
#   on its east side, the pushed stone of a sticky piston on its north side and the drop cell on
#   its south side, over the output hopper.
# - The read is a comparator in compare mode with a redstone block on its side: 15 only when
#   the box is full. Its output runs north along the third column through two repeaters and
#   back west into the stone behind the piston, which pushes its stone into X: the box breaks and
#   the moving stone pushes it into the drop cell.
# - The dispenser fires whenever its contents change (a comparator reads it through a stone;
#   two observers watch that comparator and power the stone under the dispenser), which places
#   the first box and refills after a box shortage, and after every break (an observer under the
#   first repeater of the full line, watched by an observer that powers the read-through stone).
