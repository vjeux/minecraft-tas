# Always-on fast shulker unloader for stackable items, 2 hopper minecarts over 6 hoppers (track shulker-speed, clk-item). The dispenser D above the box cell X places full boxes. Under X, two hopper minecarts rest off-rail on the rims of six hoppers (2 x 3), each cart's centre inside X's column (so each sucks X at 20 items/s) and each cart straddling 4 hoppers: a hopper pulls from every container entity whose box overlaps the cube above it, so the six hoppers drain the carts at 6 x 2.5 = 15 items/s into six item chests. The carts face 45 degrees so they never push each other. Empty detection as in the reference: comparator C reads X through a stone, repeater W squares it, subtract comparator V (side direct, rear 4 gt late through T) pulses when X becomes empty; the pulse fires sticky piston P, whose stone breaks the box and pushes the drop west into the walled drop cell over hopper K. Off-rail carts cannot be disabled, so when the box breaks they must be full: with stackable items every cart slot still holds items (the box empties into full carts and a slot takes 64 or 16 hopper pulls to empty), so they cannot take the drop; with unstackables a slot empties within a few gt and a cart swallows the drop (no unstackable badge: use the activator-rail unloader). Observer Q on the stone's cell re-fires D on the retraction; comparator E reads D through a stone and observer Q2 above it fires D whenever D's contents change (the first box, and boxes coming back after the supply ran out).
