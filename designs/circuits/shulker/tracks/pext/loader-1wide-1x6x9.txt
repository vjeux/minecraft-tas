# A one-block-wide shulker loader you can line up side by side with no gap (29 blocks, 1x6x9): a
# dispenser under the box spot places an empty box, the hopper above fills it from the items chest,
# and when the box is full a piston behind it breaks it; the dropped full box lands on the hopper
# under the box's front, which sends it to the output chest.
# How it works: the full test and the sticky-piston shuttle of the 28-block loader fire the
# breaking piston once per box. The next box no longer comes from a line along the bottom: an
# observer watching the observer above the breaking piston fires a piston on the boxes side; an
# observer sees that piston's head and fires a sticky piston at the bottom, which moves a second
# sticky piston one cell; an observer watching that cell powers the block under the box dispenser,
# which places the next box once the breaking piston has pulled back. An observer on the comparator
# that reads the boxes chest also powers that second sticky piston, which places the first box.
# Hand rebuild of the 28-block loader without its bottom row (one more column on the boxes side),
# then shrunk from 32 to 29 blocks by `mctas circuit shulker anneal --soft-volume`.
