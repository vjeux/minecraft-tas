# A one-block-wide shulker loader you can line up side by side with no gap (30 blocks, 1x6x9): a
# dispenser under the box spot places an empty box, the hopper above fills it from the items chest,
# and when the box is full a piston behind it breaks it; the dropped full box lands on the hopper
# under the box's front, which sends it to the output chest.
# How it works: the full test and the sticky-piston shuttle of the 28-block loader fire the
# breaking piston once per box. On the boxes side, the comparator on the boxes chest powers a
# redstone dust and an observer watches that dust, so every change of the chest's level (also 27
# boxes to 26, with a full chest) reaches the box dispenser: through an observer, a dispenser used
# as a block, another observer and a dispenser used as a block under the box dispenser. The
# observer above the breaking piston reaches the same dust through an observer, a sticky piston
# and the observer that sees its head, so the next box goes in once the breaking piston has pulled
# back. Hand rebuild of the 28-block loader without its bottom row (one more column on the boxes
# side), then 32 -> 30 blocks by `mctas circuit shulker anneal --soft-volume`; it passes the
# judge's full-chest check (27 boxes), which the 28-block loader failed.
