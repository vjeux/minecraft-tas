# A one-block-wide shulker loader you can line up side by side with no gap (31 blocks, 1x6x9): a
# dispenser under the box spot places an empty box, the hopper above fills it from the items chest,
# and when the box is full a piston behind it breaks it; the dropped full box lands on the hopper
# under the box's front, which sends it to the output chest.
# How it works: the full test and the sticky-piston shuttle of the 28-block loader fire the
# breaking piston once per box. Two pulses reach a dropper on the boxes side: from an observer on
# the comparator that reads the boxes chest (boxes arrive) and from an observer watching the
# observer above the breaking piston (a box was broken). The dropper fires a piston; an observer
# sees its head and fires a sticky piston at the bottom, which moves an observer one cell, out or
# back, on every pulse; the observer next to it sees the move and fires the box dispenser through
# a stone. On the very first pulse the sticky piston pushes both observers, and the moved
# observer's own pulse makes its neighbour fire a second time 4 gt later: that late fire finds the
# first box in the dispenser, so it also starts from a box chest that is kept full and never
# changes. It passes the judge's full-chest and endless-supply checks. Hand rebuild (the boxes
# chest's pulse sent through the same dropper as the after-break pulse), then 33 -> 31 blocks by
# `mctas circuit shulker anneal --soft-volume`.
