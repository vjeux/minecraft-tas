## Mechanics (26.3 source, verified in the game)

- A hopper pulls from any container ENTITY whose box overlaps the 1x1x1 cube above it (a random one each try). A hopper minecart (0.98 wide) straddling a cell edge feeds 2 hoppers; off-rail at a corner, 4.
- A hopper minecart sucks the block container at containing(x, y + 1.5, z): its centre must be inside the box's column, one cell under the box. 1 item/gt.
- With no container above, a hopper minecart sucks item entities in the 1x1 column from 0.69 to 2.0 above its own level, i.e. inside the box cell: it swallows the broken box's drop in the very tick it spawns (block events run before entities) unless it is full or disabled.
- Off-rail carts cannot be disabled. They are safe only when every slot still holds items at break time: true for 64- and 16-stack items, false for unstackables (a slot empties within a few gt).
- An activator rail powered in the same redstone update as the breaking piston disables carts on it before they tick (activateMinecart runs in the cart's tick before it sucks).
- Stationary carts keep the yaw they were spawned with. Two carts push each other only if |direction . facing| >= 0.8 within 0.2 of each other, so yaw 45 lets carts sit side by side in x or z.
- Never put a cart centre exactly on a cell edge (z = 0.0): a 90 degree turn maps it to -0.0 and the wrong column (rotations failed until 0.01).
- Loader face budget with the dispenser above the box: dispenser, piston stone, drop exit, comparator read-through and ONE feeding hopper (a hopper under the box would pull). So a hopper loader is capped at 2.5 items/s per box. More needs droppers (5/s per face, dropper under the box facing up), which must be gated: a dropper facing air throws its item. Gate idea: subtract comparator, rear = the box level L, side = a clock (pulses only while L > 0), first item from the hopper. Tried a second hopper with the drop leaving downward (loader-2h.txt): the drop escapes, no good.
- Rail unloader tiles at a 6-block pitch; the off-rail one cannot get `tileable` (the badge runs with unstackables).

## Smallest loader: tried, no gain yet

clk-rt's always-on loader (31b) is near-minimal for its architecture. Tried: comparator reading the box directly (no read-through stone), the redstone block doubling as the drop cell's north wall, 4 dust straight into the piston (29b, loader-small-directread-FAILS.txt). Fails: when the piston's stone fills the box cell, a comparator next to the cell reads THROUGH the stone into the feed hopper behind it, the hopper fills during the swap, reads 15, and the piston stays extended forever. The read-through stone exists for that reason: the comparator then reads the pushed stone itself (0). Feed hopper and comparator sit on opposite faces of the box cell (the piston and the drop exit take the other horizontal pair), so a direct read always sees the feed hopper.

## Combo (loader + unloader, `port input.mode` lever): plan

One items chest cannot be both the loader's source (above the feed hopper) and the unloader's sink (fed by the hopper under the box), and the judge fills the item chests in name order with 81 stacks, so a sink named items.b would get 27 stacks in loader mode. Plan: loader sources items.a/b/c into one pool hopper over the feed hopper, unloader sink items.d (gets no budget in loader mode); one comparator reading the box through the read stone feeds both detectors (compare with a redstone block = full; squared + subtract pulse = became empty); the mode lever locks the full-detector repeater (unloader mode) and, through a torch, the unloading hopper and the empty-pulse path (loader mode).

Better plan (one detector chain, one path to the piston): S = (L > 0) AND NOT(full AND loader mode). Build it as in the fast loader: C -> dust A (L) -> Z (subtract, rear A, side = K2's full signal through repeater f) -> W (square) = S. Then a falling-edge pulse V (subtract: side = S direct, rear = S 4 gt late) fires the piston. Loader mode: S falls when the box reaches 15 (pulse, break); when the full box breaks L goes 15 -> 0 but S was already 0 (no pulse). Unloader mode: the mode lever locks f, so S = (L > 0) and falls when the box empties. Plus a torch on the lever's block that locks the unloading hopper H in loader mode. Layout: the fast loader's west piston and its K2/B/f/Z/W block, with V near the piston.

## WIP: loader-dropper.txt (hopper N + gated dropper Y under the box)

First 1-stack run: box 1 full at t=141 (the hopper-only loader needs ~240), but the dropper fired after the box broke and threw a shovel into the output (fail). Cause: the gate G (subtract comparator looping through repeater R, rear = squared L > 0) only stops when L drops, i.e. 4-8 gt after the piston breaks the box, while dropper ticks are already scheduled. Fix plan: stop the gate at FULL, before the break: put a subtract comparator Z between A and the squaring (rear = A, side = K2's full signal) so the gate sees L only while 0 < L < 15, and delay the piston path a few gt (T delay 4) so the last scheduled dropper tick hits the full box (a dropper facing a full container keeps its item). B (K2's redstone block) must then move off Z's side and away from the piston dust.

Second run (Z + 18 gt piston delay, committed): boxes 1-2 full at t=157 and 321 with nothing in the output, then stuck: the judge shares the item budget across the sources (items.a first), so N's chest (items.a) ran dry after two boxes and could not seed box 3 (the gate needs L > 0), while the dropper chains held the rest; one item was still thrown (found resting on R), so a dropper tick still reaches an empty cell sometimes. Third run (commit after this): fixed the strays. (1) Z's side must be a repeater (a dust 2 steps from K2 arrived at 13 < L = 15, so Z kept passing 2 and the gate never stopped). (2) The dropper under the box is QC-powered by anything powering the box cell: a dust ON the dispenser gives the dispenser direct power, so the cell under it "has a neighbour signal" and the dropper fired with every dispenser trigger; fix: the dust goes one block higher on a stone over the dispenser (the dispenser is then QC-triggered without direct power). (3) The always-on dust network leaked into the seed repeater through a dust pointing into Q2's output stone; fix: a repeater instead of that dust. Now nothing is thrown, but box 3 is never seeded: Q2 only fires when comparator E goes 0 <-> >0 (an observer sees the comparator's powered state, not its level), so a placement with boxes left in the dispenser gives no seed. Fourth run: ARCHIVED as shulker-speed-loader-dropper (5.74 items/s, robust, every badge except flush and mixed). The seeding fix: N's supply must not run out before the dropper's. Three tries: (a) N with its own chest: it drains first (the judge gives each chest 1/3, and N works while the dropper waits for each new box); (b) N's source hopper fed by two chests, overflow to the dropper, plus a separate chest chain into the dropper's north face: the dropper prefers the overflow line, so N's two chests still drain first; (c) ALL three chests feed one pool hopper H1 above N (one from above, two from side hoppers), and H1's only push goes west and down into the dropper: N never starves while any chest has items. Cost: the dropper gets one feed (2.5/s), so 5-5.7/s instead of 7.5. Next for 7.5/s: a second dropper feed that only draws when N is full.
