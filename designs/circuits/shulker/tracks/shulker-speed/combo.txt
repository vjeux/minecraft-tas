# Shulker loader + unloader combo with a mode lever (track shulker-speed, clk-item). Lever off: a loader; lever on: an unloader, on the same chests. Core: clk-rt's always-on loader (dispenser D above the box cell X; observer Q on the pushed stone's cell re-fires D; comparator E + observer Q2 place the first box and restart after the supply ran out; sticky piston P's stone breaks the box and pushes the drop into the walled cell over the collection hopper K). Loader feed: chests a, b, c into one pool hopper over the feed hopper N. Unloader: hopper H under X empties it into chest d. The judge's 81-stack loader budget fills a, b, c in name order, so d stays empty in loader mode. One detector serves both modes. Comparator C reads X's level L into dust A, and K2 (compare, redstone block beside it) gives 15 only at L = 15. Z subtracts K2's signal (through repeater F, 8 gt) from A (through repeater R1, 2 gt), and repeater W squares it: S = (0 < L < 15). Because the full signal arrives 6 gt late, a box that is filled to 14 at once and topped up a gt later still makes S rise. V subtracts S (through repeater f) from S delayed (T2) and pulses for 4 gt when S falls: when a loading box gets full, or when an unloading box gets empty. P fires from V directly. The mode lever (on its floor) locks F through two repeaters, so a full box arriving in unloader mode never fires the piston. The lever's floor also powers a dust line under the build to the block holding the wall torch under H, so the torch (which locks H) goes off and H unloads only in unloader mode.
