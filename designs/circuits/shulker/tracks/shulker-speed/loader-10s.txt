# 10 items/s shulker loader (track shulker-speed, clk-item): two droppers fill one box. Dropper Y2 beside the box cell X is fired by two observers in opposite phases of the gate's 8 gt clock, so it dispenses every 4 gt (5 items/s); an observer firing into Y2 also makes Y2 a strongly powered solid block, so the box cell gets a neighbour signal and dropper Y1 UNDER X fires by quasi-connectivity in the same tick, for another 5 items/s. Each dropper is fed 5 items/s by two hoppers, and the supply comes from two item chests, each over a hopper minecart that straddles two hopper cells (a cart pulls 20 items/s, two hoppers under it take 5 items/s). Gate: comparator C reads X's level L through a stone into dust A; K2 (compare, redstone block beside it) gives 15 only at L = 15; subtract comparator Z passes L only while 0 < L < 15, repeater W squares it, and subtract comparator G loops through repeater R into its own side, an 8 gt clock while the gate is open. Always-on: comparator E reads the dispenser D and observer Q2 fires D whenever D's contents change, so the first box is placed by itself and boxes come back after the supply ran out. The piston P breaks a full box and its stone pushes the drop into the cell over the collection hopper K.
