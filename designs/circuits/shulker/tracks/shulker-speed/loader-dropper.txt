# Fast shulker loader, hopper + gated dropper (track shulker-speed, clk-item), WIP. The always-on loader (dispenser D above the box cell X, hopper N feeding X from the south, sticky piston P whose stone breaks the full box and pushes the drop into the walled cell over hopper K) plus a dropper Y under X facing up, fed by two hopper chains. Comparator C reads X's raw level L through a stone into dust A; K2 (compare, redstone block B on its side) passes only L = 15 to the piston; repeater W squares L > 0 down to G, a subtract comparator whose output loops back through repeater R into its side: it oscillates while L > 0 and stops when X is empty or gone, and observer Ob under Y fires the dropper on every change of G. So the dropper never fires at an empty cell, the hopper puts the first item into each new box, and the design rests at the end.
