# Fast always-on shulker loader, hopper + gated dropper, 5.7 items/s (2.3x the hopper loader; track shulker-speed, clk-item). The dispenser D above the box cell X places empty boxes; a sticky piston P breaks a full box and its stone pushes the drop into the walled cell over the collection hopper K. Items: all three item chests feed one pool hopper H1 (one from above, two through side hoppers) above hopper N, which feeds X from the south; H1's overflow runs west and down two hoppers into a dropper Y under X facing up. N never starves while any chest has items, so it gives every new box its first item. Gate: comparator C reads X's level L through a stone into dust A; K2 (compare, redstone block beside it) passes only a full box, to the piston through two delay-4 repeaters (18 gt) and through repeater f onto the side of subtract comparator Z, so Z passes L only while 0 < L < 15. Repeater W squares Z's output down to G, a subtract comparator looping through repeater R into its own side: it oscillates while its rear is on, and observer Ob under Y fires the dropper on each change. The dropper therefore never fires at an empty cell, a breaking box or air (a dropper facing a full box keeps its item). The dispenser is powered through a stone under the always-on dust so the dropper is not QC-fired by it. Always-on: comparator E reads D and observer Q2 fires D when D's contents go between empty and not.
