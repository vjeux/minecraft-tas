# Fast always-on shulker loader with restart AND flush, 7.4 items/s (track shulker-speed, clk-item). It is shulker-speed-loader-dropper75 plus two additions. (1) A placement one-shot. Comparator A on the dispenser outputs into a dust line, and an observer at its far end fires whenever the dispenser level changes (a box placed or arriving). The observer strongly powers the drop-cell wall stone. The dot dust on top of the collection hopper then points into the box cell and powers the closed box, which is a conductor, so the bottom dropper fires once by quasi-connectivity. A new box thus gets its first item even when the leftovers sit in the dropper rather than in the feed hopper; the 0<L<15 gate needs that first item. (2) An hourglass flush. Chest Tc holds 500 stone over hopper Hd, which drains it into a sink only while item chest b is empty (comparator CH) and the dispenser holds boxes. For the second condition, a wall torch on the dispenser dust floor lights when the dispenser is empty and joins the lock line. Draining takes 4000 gt: longer than the restart pause (the judge tops every source chest up each tick, so the chests run dry together) and within the flush deadline. The hourglass is frozen once the spare box is placed. When Tc runs empty, comparator HG turns off and the wall torch on its output stone lights. Repeater I then powers the read stone of the box comparator: a fake full fires the piston and closes the gate.
