# Mixed-item shulker loader with restart (track shulker-speed, clk-item): the mixed loader plus a persistence filter, so the same jam detector survives the restart badge. Comparator H reads the feeding hopper N. Subtract comparator T1 takes H minus a composter at level 7, so transient backlogs are ignored and only a deep one (N at 3 or more slots) passes. T1's output runs through 8 delay-4 repeaters, a stone and a wall torch (32 gt and inverted) into the side of subtract comparator T2, whose rear is T1 undelayed: T2 is AND(now, 32 gt ago), so a backlog shorter than 32 gt never reaches the piston. A box swap fills N for a handful of ticks (measured: 2 gt at the top level in the restart run), while a jam holds it forever. T2 feeds the old edge detector: subtract comparator Vj pulses for 4 gt on T2's rise (its side gets the same signal 4 gt late through repeater X), and the pulse powers, through a dust and a stone, the sticky piston that breaks the box.
