# Shulker loader with both restart and flush (track shulker-speed, clk-item): clk-rt's always-on loader plus an hourglass flush that outlasts the restart pause. The hourglass is a chest Tc preloaded with 420 stone items (not a scenario item) over hopper Hd, which drains it into a sink chest only while the judge's items chest is empty AND the dispenser still holds boxes. Comparator CH reads the items chest and feeds repeater Rc. The dispenser comparator E drives repeater Y into a stone, and the wall torch on that stone is lit when the dispenser is empty. Both feed the dust line that locks Hd, through the floor under its last dust. Draining 420 items takes 3360 gt: longer than the restart pause (3000 gt) and within the flush deadline (6000 gt). It stays frozen once the dispenser is empty, so the spare box at the end of a normal run never starts it. When Tc runs empty, comparator HG turns off and the wall torch on its output stone lights a dust line. Subtract comparator Vj turns its rising edge into a 4 gt pulse (its side gets the line 4 gt late through repeater X), and the pulse fires the sticky piston: the last partial box leaves.
