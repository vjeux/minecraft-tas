# Small always-on shulker loader (track shulker-speed, clk-item): clk-rt's always-on loader with its full detector rebuilt. Comparator C sits right next to the box cell and reads the box directly (no read-through stone), and the redstone block B on its side, which gates it to 15 (full), doubles as the drop cell's north wall. C's output runs along four dust straight into the sticky piston, with no repeater and no stone. The rest is the always-on loader: the dispenser above the box cell, a hopper feeding it from the south, the sticky piston two cells away pushing a stone into the box, the moving stone pushing the drop into the walled cell over the collection hopper, an observer on the pushed stone's cell firing the dispenser after each box, and a comparator reading the dispenser through the drop cell's roof, with an observer that fires the dispenser when boxes arrive.
