# Two-station rail unloader (track shulker-speed, clk-item): two copies of shulker-speed-unloader-rail (copy B 6 blocks south of copy A) share one boxes chest through a hopper splitter, so two boxes empty in parallel. Splitter: hopper Hs under the boxes chest pushes north along a hopper line into copy A's box hopper; hopper U2 under Hs pulls from it and pushes south along a second line into copy B's box hopper. Boxes therefore alternate between the two dispensers. Each copy: two hopper minecarts on one activator rail under the box (one at each end of the rail cell) pull the box at 20 items/s and feed three hoppers (7.5/s); its comparator reads the box through a stone and fires the sticky piston when the box empties; the same pulse powers the activator rail so the carts do not swallow the broken box.
