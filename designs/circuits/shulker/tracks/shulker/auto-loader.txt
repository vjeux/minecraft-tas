# Always-on shulker loader (track shulker, hand-built), the reference loader with its start lever
# replaced by a comparator that reads the dispenser through the drop cell's roof. An observer above
# that comparator fires the dispenser whenever it goes from holding no box to holding one: it
# places the first box by itself, and again whenever boxes come back after the supply ran out
# (badges always-on and box-starved). The rest is the reference loader: the dispenser above the
# box cell, a hopper feeding it from the side, a comparator in compare mode with a redstone block on
# its side (15 only when full), a sticky piston two cells away pushing a stone into the box, the
# moving stone pushing the drop into a walled cell over the collection hopper, and an observer on
# the pushed stone's cell firing the dispenser after each box.
