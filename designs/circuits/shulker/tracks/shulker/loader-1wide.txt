# Draft (clk-rt, 2026-10-20): a 1-wide shulker loader (pitch 1).
# - Box cell X: feed hopper above, dispenser below (facing up), piston on the north, comparator Q
#   on the south reading X. Q stands on the collector hopper K: the piston pushes the dropped box
#   over Q into K's pickup area.
# - Full is tested exactly with a read-through comparator R:
#   - Q, then Q2, carry the box level into a dust on a stone B. The copies share that dust line,
#     but a neighbour only reaches it at 14 or less, so B is at 15 only when this copy's box is full.
#   - R reads B and, through B, the empty dropper C behind it: 15 when B is at 15, else 0.
# - R toggles a copper bulb. A comparator reads the bulb, and an observer line over the top pulses
#   the piston on each change of that comparator: once per full box.
# - An observer under R pulses a line along the bottom into a lamp under the dispenser, and the
#   observer under the dispenser fires it. It fires on every change of R, so the next box is
#   placed once the piston has retracted.
# - A comparator on the second boxes hopper pulses the same lamp whenever boxes pass, which places
#   the first box and refills after a box shortage.
