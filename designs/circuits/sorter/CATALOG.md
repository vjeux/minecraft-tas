| [sorter-classic](archive/sorter-classic.txt) | sorter iron_ingot | items/s 2.50, first item by 20 gt, hoppers awake 2, slice width 1, badges overflow-proof, bill 15 iron, 1 quartz, 10 redstone | 15 | 1x5x5 | robust | Takes one item kind out of a line of mixed items into its chest, 15 blocks a slice: slices stand side by side, one kind each, at full hopper speed (2.5 items/s), and a full chest never jams the line or drains a neighbour. Recipe (every item is required, none is a test load: 105 iron ingots and 8 papers in all): the filter hopper (under the line hopper) gets 41 of the item to sort in its first slot and 1 paper renamed "filter" in each of its 4 other slots (any item that never comes down the line); the drain hopper under it gets 64 of the item in its first slot and 4 items that cannot stack with the filter's fillers (here papers renamed "lock"). 16-stack items (snowballs, ender pearls): 10 in the filter and 16 in the drain, the same fillers (designs/circuits/tracks/sorter/sorter-classic-16.txt, also judged with both badges). It cannot sort non-stackable items (tools, armour: they pass through) nor items whose name or enchantments differ from the recipe's. How it works: one more item in the filter raises its comparator signal from 2 to 3, the only level that gets through three dust to the repeater, which powers the torch's block; the torch (hung under the comparator's block, which locks the drain) turns off, and the full drain drops one item into the chest and takes one from the filter. Catalog parts: the NOT gate (a torch on a block) as the lock, a 2 gt repeater. |
