| [sorter-classic](archive/sorter-classic.txt) | sorter iron_ingot | items/s 2.50, first item by 20 gt, hoppers awake 2, slice width 1, badges overflow-proof, bill 15 iron, 1 quartz, 10 redstone | 15 | 1x5x5 | robust | One slice per item kind, slices side by side under a line of hoppers: the hopper under the line holds 41 of its item and 4 renamed fillers, so each extra one of that item it pulls in lets its comparator's signal reach the torch that locks the hopper below, which drops that item into the chest; everything else rides on along the line, even when a chest is full. Details: the recipe (every item is required, none is a test load: 105 iron ingots and 8 papers in all): the filter hopper (under the line hopper) gets 41 of the item to sort in its first slot and 1 paper renamed "filter" in each of its 4 other slots (any item that never comes down the line); the drain hopper under it gets 64 of the item in its first slot and 4 items that cannot stack with the filter's fillers (here papers renamed "lock"). 16-stack items (snowballs, ender pearls): 10 in the filter and 16 in the drain, the same fillers. It cannot sort items that do not stack (tools, armour: they pass through) nor items renamed or enchanted unlike the recipe's. End a water stream on a hopper of the line before the first slice, not on the first slice itself. |
