# CIRCUIT: stairs-stone-4 piston-stairs 4 steps 4 up, width 1 cell, extend 3 gt, retract 3 gt, material stone, flips safe (the lever flipped back at any time), 14 blocks, 4x5x6, robust
# A hidden stone staircase of 4 steps (14 blocks, 4x5x6): flip the lever on and 4 stone steps slide
# out of the side wall, one block up each, from the floor to a ledge 4 blocks higher; flip it off and
# they slide back flush into the wall, where they look like the wall's own stone.
# How it works: behind each stone step is a slime block, and one more slime block under each of those
# from the second step on joins them into one sticky piece; one sticky piston behind it pushes the
# slime, which pushes the steps out, and pulls it back, the slime pulling each stone home (stone does
# not stick to stone). The piston moves 11 blocks (at most 12). Stone steps walk like normal ground
# (pext).
name: stairs-stone-4
circuit: piston-stairs 4
travel: 0 0 1
port stairs: 0 0 0
port input: -4 1 1 lever
origin: -4 0 -1
