# CIRCUIT: stairs-stone-5 piston-stairs 5 steps 5 up, width 1 cell, extend 3 gt, retract 3 gt, material stone, flips safe (the lever flipped back at any time), 18 blocks, 4x6x7, robust
# A hidden stone staircase of 5 steps (18 blocks, 4x6x7): flip the lever on and 5 stone steps slide
# out of the side wall, one block up each, from the floor to a ledge 5 blocks higher; flip it off and
# they slide back flush into the wall, where they look like the wall's own stone.
# How it works: behind each stone step is a slime block, and one more slime block under each of those
# joins them into sticky pieces; a sticky piston behind them pushes the slime, which pushes the steps
# out, and pulls it back, the slime pulling each stone home (stone does not stick to stone). One
# piston moves at most 12 blocks, so the staircase is two pieces (the first 3 steps, the last 2: the
# gap under the fourth step's slime keeps them apart), each with its own piston; the lever stands on
# a stone block touching both pistons (no slime touches it). Stone steps walk like normal ground
# (pext).
name: stairs-stone-5
circuit: piston-stairs 5
travel: 0 0 1
port stairs: 0 0 0
port input: -4 2 3 lever
origin: -4 0 -1
