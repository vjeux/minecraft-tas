# A hidden staircase of 5 steps (12 blocks, 3x6x7): flip the lever on and 5 slime-block steps slide
# out of the side wall, one block up each, from the floor to a ledge 5 blocks higher; flip it off and
# they slide back flush into the wall.
# How it works: the steps are slime blocks, joined by one more slime block under each step from the
# second on, so the whole staircase is one sticky piece of 9 blocks that one sticky piston behind it
# pushes out and pulls back. The ledge at the top is obsidian, which slime cannot pull (another block
# there would be dragged into the wall with the steps). Slime steps bounce a fall and slow a walker:
# the stone staircase walks like normal ground (pext).
