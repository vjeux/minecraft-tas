# A hidden stone staircase of 3 steps (11 blocks, 4x4x5): flip the lever on and 3 stone steps slide

# they slide back flush into the wall, where they look like the wall's own stone.

# from the second step on joins them into one sticky piece; one sticky piston behind it pushes the
# slime, which pushes the steps out, and pulls it back, the slime pulling each stone home (stone does
# not stick to stone). Stone steps walk like normal ground (pext).
