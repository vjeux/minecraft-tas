# CIRCUIT: tff-crafter7 tff latency 21 gt, edge rising, min-pulse 2 gt (the shortest a real input makes), min-period over 30 gt, 7 blocks, 3x2x2, robust
# Crafter T flip-flop, 7 blocks (track tff): a crafter holding nine iron ingots faces a hopper that feeds back into it. A pulse crafts an iron block into the hopper, which hands it back 8 gt later; the next pulse crafts the block back into nine ingots, which the hopper returns one by one (the crafter spreads them over its nine slots). A subtract comparator reads the crafter (filled slots: 9 or 1) minus a constant 1 (a comparator reading a dropper that holds one stone), so the output is 8 or 0 once the ingots are back.
