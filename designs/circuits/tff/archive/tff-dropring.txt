# CIRCUIT: tff-dropring tff latency 13 gt, edge rising, min-pulse 2 gt (the shortest a real input makes), min-period 12 gt, 8 blocks, 3x3x2, robust
# Dropper ring T flip-flop, 8 blocks (track tff): droppers D1 and D2 at opposite corners of a 2x2 ring, each passing to a hopper that passes on to the other dropper; one stone. The input fires both droppers (D1 directly, D2 through two dust on the hopper and on D2); only the one holding the stone moves it, into its hopper, which delivers it to the other dropper 7 gt later, after both have fired, so the order in which the two droppers fire never matters (two droppers facing each other would pass the stone back in the same tick). A comparator reads D2. Latency 13 gt, 1 gt pulses, trains down to 12 gt.
