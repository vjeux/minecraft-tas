# CIRCUIT: tff-observer-flat tff latency 7 gt, edge falling, min-pulse 3 gt, min-period 10 gt, 5 blocks, 3x1x3, robust
# Observer + slime + two sticky pistons T flip-flop, flat, 5 blocks (found by the tff census): sticky piston s (next to the input, facing south) has a redstone block R in front of it; an observer watching s fires a second sticky piston A (through the slime block it outputs into) on both edges of each pulse. One pulse leaves R in front of s (output off), the next has A pull R over the output cell (on), whose next pulse pushes it back. All in one layer. Latency 7 gt (on the falling edge), 3 gt pulses and longer, trains down to a 10 gt period.
