# CIRCUIT: tff-observer-piston tff latency 11 gt, edge rising, min-pulse 3 gt, min-period 10 gt, 4 blocks, 3x3x1, robust
# Observer + sticky piston T flip-flop, 4 blocks (found by the tff census): a sticky piston P facing down (powered by the input) has an observer O watching it from the east, and a sticky piston Q two cells east of P's foot faces west at a redstone block R; Q is quasi-connected through the cell O outputs into. Each input pulse makes P extend and retract, so O pulses twice: with R next to Q (output on), Q's first short pulse pushes R under P and spits it there; with R under P, P pushes it down and pulls it back, Q's head reaches out and the second observer pulse pulls R home. Latency 11 gt, pulses of 3 gt and longer, trains down to a 10 gt period; rests ON.
