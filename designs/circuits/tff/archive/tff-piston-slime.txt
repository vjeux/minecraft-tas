# CIRCUIT: tff-piston-slime tff latency 4 gt, edge rising, min-pulse 2 gt (the shortest a real input makes), min-period 5 gt, 5 blocks, 4x2x1, robust
# Piston + slime T flip-flop, 5 blocks (found by the tff census): sticky piston s (facing east, next to the input) has a slime block and a redstone block R in front; a piston p under R faces back west (R powers it: extended at rest) and a piston P under s faces east (quasi-powered by the input through s's cell). ON: s pushes slime + R east (R over the output cell), p loses R and retracts, P extends into the gap; when the input falls s cannot pull the slime back (it would drag p into P's head), P retracts and p, now quasi-powered through the slime, extends again. OFF: s extends into the empty cell and, on the falling edge, pulls slime + R back (the extended p is immovable and stays). Latency 5 gt, 1 gt pulses, trains down to a 5 gt period.
name: tff-piston-slime
