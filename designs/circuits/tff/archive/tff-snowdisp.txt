# CIRCUIT: tff-snowdisp tff latency 6 gt, edge rising, min-pulse 1 gt, min-period 4 gt, 6 blocks, 2x3x3, robust
# Powder snow dispenser T flip-flop, 6 blocks (track tff): each input pulse fires a dispenser facing up; it holds a powder snow bucket (it places powder snow above itself and keeps an empty bucket) or an empty bucket (it picks the powder snow back up). Comparator C reads the dispenser: 2 with the powder snow bucket (stack size 1), 1 with the empty bucket (stack size 16); comparator K feeds C's side a constant 2 read from a dropper holding a powder snow bucket, so C (compare mode) gives 2 or 0. Latency 6 gt (dispenser 4 + comparator 2), 1 gt pulses, trains down to 4 gt; rests ON.
