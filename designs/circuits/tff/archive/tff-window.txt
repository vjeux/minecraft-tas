# CIRCUIT: tff-window tff latency 4 gt, edge falling, min-pulse 2 gt, min-period 4 gt, 18 blocks, 3x2x6, robust
# Single-latch pulse-window T flip-flop, redstone only, 18 blocks (track tff, shrunk from the master-slave by a lock census): repeater M holds q; its lock C (a comparator) reads a dust A that the input powers while on and that a stone powers while off (through a subtract comparator with a redstone block behind it: NOT input). The stone takes 2 gt to come back after the falling edge, so A and C drop for one short window, and M copies its own inverse once (M's output stone B, B's torch, three dust back into M). Latency 4 gt (falling edge), 2 gt pulses, trains down to a 4 gt period.
