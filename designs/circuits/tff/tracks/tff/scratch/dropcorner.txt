# Dropper + hopper T flip-flop without dust, 8 blocks (track tff): the input cell touches two droppers (east and above). The stone goes from the east dropper up into a hopper that hands it to the upper dropper, and from the upper dropper through three hoppers back down to the east one; each pulse fires both droppers, only the one holding the stone moves it, and it always lands after both have fired. A comparator reads the east dropper.
