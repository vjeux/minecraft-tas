# CIRCUIT: tnt-airburst-lever-56b tnt-duper rate 22.2 TNT/min, period 54 gt, per period 1, runs lever on, switch lever off: stops within 12 gt; on: restarts within 53 gt, safe drop 0 blocks (on the ground), 56 blocks, 5x5x11, robust (survives its own explosions: 24 exploded, design intact; precise: 24 TNT at one point at fuse 1, in mid-air; launched at (-0.0024, 0.1568, -1.0000) blocks/gt, spread (0.0378, 0.0000, 0.0000); useful: every blast clear of fluids, each destroys blocks on open ground)
# PRECISE AIR BURST with an on/off LEVER (vjeux 2026-10-21: "Can we have an on off switch for all the tnt"; coordinator:
# clean stop, nothing left on the launcher). tnt-airburst's engine, P54 clock, water channel and slime launcher, with
# two changes. (1) Each TNT's launch no longer waits for the NEXT engine push (a stop stranded the last TNT on the
# launcher): the engine's obsidian, powered by its dust each time the frame comes back (once per TNT), fires a dropper
# into a ring of 11 hoppers whose comparator, 63 gt after the prime, powers the launcher's repeater (5 items circling,
# 7 gt a hopper). (2) The lever: a torch on a wall behind the clock's downward sticky piston holds it while the lever
# is off (the lever under the torch's stone turns the torch off); built as the lever leaves it stopped (the clock's
# pistons extended, the engine piston extended). Lever off (as built): nothing moves, nothing primed. Lever on: one
# TNT per 54 gt (22.2 TNT/min), each launched up to one point in mid-air above the machine, which stands on the ground.
# Lever off: the clock stops within a cycle and the TNT on its way is still launched on time.
