# CIRCUIT: tnt-precise-drop-lever-52b tnt-duper rate 30 TNT/min, period 40 gt, per period 1, runs lever on, switch lever off: stops within 5 gt; on: restarts within 27 gt, safe drop 5 blocks, stands 23 blocks above the ground (stand: in the design), 52 blocks, 6x5x8, robust (survives its own explosions: 33 exploded, design intact; precise: 33 TNT at one point at fuse 1, in mid-air 9.1 blocks below the machine, 13.9 blocks above the ground; launched at (0.0007, 0.1568, -1.0000) blocks/gt, spread (0.0387, 0.0000, 0.0000); dropped: every TNT falls straight down, 12.3+ blocks by fuse 1; useful: every blast clear of fluids, each destroys blocks on open ground)
# PRECISE DROP with an on/off LEVER (vjeux 2026-10-21: "Can we have an on off switch for all the tnt"; coordinator:
# clean stop, nothing left in the channel). tnt-precise-drop's engine, P40 clock, water aligner and trapdoor, with two
# changes. (1) The release of each TNT no longer waits for the NEXT engine push (that stranded the last TNT in the water
# whenever the clock stopped): the engine's torch, relighting at each prime, fires a dropper into a ring of 11 hoppers
# whose comparator opens the trapdoor 53 gt later (one item per TNT, 4 items circling, 7 gt per hopper), so every TNT
# already has its release on the way when the machine stops. (2) The lever: a torch standing next to the clock's
# downward sticky piston holds it while the lever is off (the lever on the torch's stone turns the torch off). Lever
# off (as built): nothing moves, nothing primed. Lever on: the clock runs, one TNT per 40 gt (30 TNT/min), each at
# one point in mid-air 9.1 blocks under the machine. Lever off again: the clock stops within a cycle and the TNT
# still in the channel drops on time. Stands 23 blocks up (stand: the burst in mid-air, 13.9 blocks above the ground).
