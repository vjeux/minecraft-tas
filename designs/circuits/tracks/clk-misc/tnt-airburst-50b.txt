# Precise air burst from catalog parts (vjeux 01:00Z: "massively simplify the block count"), 50 blocks
# (was 58), 22.2 TNT/min (was 13.2): every lit TNT explodes at exactly the same point in the air,
# almost 9 blocks above the channel, bit for bit, safe on the ground. The 16b engine is held by a
# redstone torch; a 7-block catalog clock (clk-obs-p54-lamp-obs-pis-stk-7b-7b1b02) gives a 2-tick
# pulse every 54 ticks, a repeater stretches it into the torch's block and the torch goes dark for 4
# ticks: one TNT per period. Each TNT is thrown 4 blocks north into fence posts that stop it over a
# two-block water channel; the current pushes it east against a fence post standing on a slime block
# (that cancels the random sideways kick every TNT gets when it is lit). The next push of the engine
# is seen by an observer next to the frame; four more observers and a repeater (it stretches the
# 2-tick observer pulse, which a sticky piston answers by dropping its block) carry it to a sticky
# piston under the slime, which launches the TNT straight up 64 ticks after it was lit, so it
# explodes near the top of its flight, more than 7 blocks from anything a blast could break.
