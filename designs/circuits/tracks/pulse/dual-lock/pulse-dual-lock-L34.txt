name: pulse-dual-lock-L34
# Dual-edge one-shot (L = 34 gt on each edge): an observer watching the input fires on both edges through a repeater into a copper bulb (lit on a rising edge of its power); a comparator reads the bulb and feeds a lock (a repeater pointing into the trigger repeater's side: triggers are ignored while the bulb is lit, so short inputs cannot latch it) and a U of delay-4 repeaters (2 rows) back into the bulb, which turns it off one loop later. The loop keeps the bulb powered a loop longer, so the falling edge is answered only for inputs of 80 gt or more (generator: mctas circuit pulse family dual-lock --n 1).
circuit: dual-edge
origin: 1 0 -3
port input: 2 1 3
port output: 3 1 0
