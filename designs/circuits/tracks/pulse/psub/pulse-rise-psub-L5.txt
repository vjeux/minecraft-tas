# Odd pulses (rising-edge, L = 5 gt): a subtract comparator's rear gets a redstone block that a sticky piston pushes behind it (the piston moves in the tick after the input changes and the block lands in the block-entity phase: an odd delay), its side gets the input through two repeaters (delays 2 and 2: an even delay); the output is on from the rear's arrival until the side's: L = 2 x (2 + 2) - 3 = 5 gt. A 1 gt input makes the piston leave its block behind the comparator until the next input pulls it back.
