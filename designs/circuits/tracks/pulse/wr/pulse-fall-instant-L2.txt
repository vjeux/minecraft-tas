name: pulse-fall-instant-L2
circuit: falling-edge
# The fastest falling-edge detector: a 2-tick pulse in the very tick the input turns off (delay 0),
# 14 blocks. A stone sits on one of two dust steps of a line and the input's sticky piston moves it
# to the other step and back; while it moves (a moving block conducts nothing) both steps are open.
# The line is fed by a repeater reading the input, so it carries power only from 4 ticks after the
# input turns on to 4 ticks after it turns off: the move on the falling edge lights the output, the
# move on the rising edge finds the line dead. Detail: the dust beside the input cell powers the
# piston at once; inputs of 4 ticks or more.
origin: 0 0 -2
port input: 0 1 -2
port output: 1 1 3
