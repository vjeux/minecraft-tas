# Latch hopper clock (clk-item, draft): two hoppers A -> B / B -> A pass N stones back and forth; a waxed copper bulb remembers the direction. The comparators reading A and B light torches TA / TB when their hopper is EMPTY; TA (under the bulb) and TB (under stone W beside the bulb) toggle the bulb on each source-empty edge. A comparator reading the bulb lights a dust on the stone above A (locks A); the wall torch on that stone locks B when the bulb is off. P ~ 16 N.
