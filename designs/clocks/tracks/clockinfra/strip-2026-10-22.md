# clock strip, 805 slop clocks

vjeux, 2026-10-22: "I counted 19 of these clocks that are actually just basic observer clocks but
with a bunch of unnecessary blocks around them. A clock archive shouldn't be filled with
unnecessary clock slop." The rule: an archived clock must be IRREDUCIBLE. `mctas clock strip`
removes blocks on redsim, one at a time to a fixed point, then face-adjacent pairs, keeping a
removal only when the rest gives a builder the same: the builder period (one rising edge per
period, or the even spacing of several) and pulse widths at the declared output, a usable output
when the original's is, no new open hopper top, a sculk clock no less vibration-proof; ports and
the kick's block stay. Run over the 2384 shown archived clocks: 805 strip smaller, 1501 are
irreducible, 78 were skipped (extendable family bases, designs redsim cannot run).

`clock strip --apply` (2026-10-22): 738 slop clocks removed, each because an irreducible archived
clock gives a builder as much (same builder period and pulses, as robust, usable, no worse with
items, flat, vibration-proof) with no more blocks than its core; 33 replaced by their core, judged
in the game first (robust, usable, items as the original); 34 kept, all with entities (boat clocks:
redsim's strip is no evidence there). Every removal is listed in archive/aliases.tsv under the
clock that stays. The shown set went from 2384 to 1646 clocks; 46 builder periods lost clocks (P6
312 -> 72, P8 159 -> 77, P4 123 -> 57, ...); 5 records changed, each to a smaller core: P4 (5 -> 4
blocks), P20 (6 -> 5), P25 (4 -> 3), P1446 and P6926 (11 -> 10). `clock archive` now refuses slop.

Most slop (757) had several output edges per cycle: a fast observer clock whose long state cycle
came from blocks that never reach the output (under the one-edge rule it is that fast clock).

By track: census 79, clk-item 3, clk-misc 35, clk-obs 616, clk-sculk 65, clockinfra 7.

## The game and the families (2026-10-23)

`clock strip --game`: the 73 shown clocks with entities or fluids (boats, carts, water), stripped
in the game: each removal screened on the base build, then the full clock judge over positions and
rotations, redsim's removals tried first. 34 slop (every boat ring, boat lift and boat bouncer
redsim had flagged: the game confirms each removal set; 11 stopped at the 15-minute deadline with
234 blocks left untried), 39 irreducible (the minecart shuttles, the dripleaf stand...). Applied:
31 replaced by their core (judged in the game again by `clock archive`), 3 removed (another core as
good with no more blocks). The boat lifts lose 34 to 92 blocks each (water and stone that never
mattered: l4h6 169 -> 77), the boat rings 11 to 27 (stone around the track), the bouncers 2.

`clock strip --family-check`: the 78 extendable family bases, each removal kept only when every
member n_lo..=n_hi still works without it (generated by its generator, checked at the family's own
period). 1 slop: clk-rt-div-fade (a repeater and a stone no member needs; its base replaced by
clk-rt-div-fade-core, 34 blocks, `extend remove` drops them from every generated member, the game
judged n = 1, 2, 3, 5). Not slop by this check but broken: clk-obs-divider's declared output (the
base's, and every member's exterior pick) pulses every 6 gt, the observer pair's, not once per
12·2^n gt: no air cell gets the divided signal (its chain ends in a bulb with no tap). The door
rings were false positives of the first pass (a door's lower half without its upper one): strip now
removes both halves together.

By output: one edge per cycle 48, several edges per cycle 757.
