name: gadget-landing-next-tick
# A block that LANDS acts on pistons one tick later: lever L fires piston P in tick 1; redstone
# block R is a moving piston until the block-entity phase of tick 3 (PistonMovingBlockEntity.tick:
# progress 0 -> 0.5 -> 1, placed on the third block-entity tick). The block-entity phase comes
# after the block-event phase (ServerLevel.tick), so piston U next to R's landing cell queues its
# event at tick 3 and extends in tick 4; repeater Q reading the same cell schedules 2 gt from the
# landing: on in tick 5. A k1 piston thus seeds odd scheduled ticks (k3 + 2d) but makes pistons
# act at k4 (even). Spitting R (sticky pusher retracting while R moves) places it in the
# block-event phase instead and saves that tick (gadget spit-saves-tick).
origin: -2 0 -1
palette:
  S minecraft:stone
  L minecraft:lever[face=floor,facing=east,powered=false]
  P minecraft:piston[extended=false,facing=east]
  R minecraft:redstone_block
  U minecraft:piston[extended=false,facing=south]
  Q minecraft:repeater[delay=1,facing=south,locked=false,powered=false]
  K minecraft:stone
layer 0:
  ...S
  S
layer 1:
  ...Q
  LPR.
  ...U
  ...K
