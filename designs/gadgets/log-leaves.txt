name: gadget-log-leaves
# Asymmetric odd delay (pareto-mid): persistent leaves re-read their distance in a scheduled tick
# 1 gt after a neighbor changes (LeavesBlock.updateShape -> scheduleTick(1)) and never random-tick.
# Lever L powers sticky piston P, which moves oak log G one cell east at k1 (back at k1 on the off
# flip, landing k3). Leaves A (2,1,-1) touch only the log's REST cell: they change at k2 when the
# log leaves (on flip) and at k4 after it lands back (off flip: a moving block reads as distance
# 7). Leaves B (3,1,1) touch only the cell the log moves INTO: k4 on the on flip (it lands at k3),
# k2 on the off flip. Observers OA / OB watching them light dust DA / DB 2 gt later.
