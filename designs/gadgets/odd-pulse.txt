# redstone block R off cell A at k1 (the lever acts between ticks, its pistons in tick 1). Repeater
# Rp (delay 1) reads A and falls at k1+2 = k3 (odd); the note block N next to its output block
# turns that power edge into a state change; observer O watching N fires k5..k7 into dust D.
# On the off flip P pulls R back (lands k3 BE), Rp rises at k5 and O fires k7..k9.
