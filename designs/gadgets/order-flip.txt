name: gadget-order-flip
# One 2gt pulse fires two sticky pistons that act in OPPOSITE orders on the two lever edges
# (fresh-e's e1 door mechanism). Observer O (watching the lever on top of it) strongly powers K;
# P1 is PUSH-type (stone S1 right in front of its face), P2 is PULL-type (a gap, then stone S2).
# Opening: P1 pushes S1 at once (and spits it), P2 only pulls S2 when it retracts, 2gt later.
# Closing: P2 pushes S2 back at once (spit), P1 only pulls S1 back when it retracts, 2gt later.
# So a structure that must leave first on open and come back last on close rides a push-type
# piston, and its partner rides a pull-type piston, with no extra wiring for the order.
origin: 0 0 -1
palette:
  S minecraft:stone
  K minecraft:stone
  O minecraft:observer[facing=up,powered=false]
  L minecraft:lever[face=floor,facing=east,powered=false]
  W minecraft:sticky_piston[extended=false,facing=west]
  E minecraft:sticky_piston[extended=false,facing=east]
layer 1:
  S.W
  ..K
  ..ES
layer 2:
  .
  ..O
layer 3:
  .
  ..L
