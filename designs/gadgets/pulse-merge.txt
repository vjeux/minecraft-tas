name: gadget-pulse-merge
# Pulse merger (flat-seam e89, read in seamless's flat-b-v7-full): an observer watching a delay-2
# repeater turns a DOUBLE pulse (two 2 gt pulses up to 4 gt apart: an observer watching a
# pull-type piston's gap, or a piston that extends and retracts) into exactly ONE 2 gt pulse.
# The repeater stays on for exactly 4 gt (its scheduled off is not delayed by the second input
# pulse), and its off edge comes while the watching observer is still on (the repeater's tick
# has the higher priority), so that observer fires once.
# Lever L on S; observer O watches the lever and pulses the sticky piston P (facing up, gap G,
# block K): W watches the gap and fires twice per flip; stone A, repeater R (delay 2) on stone F,
# observer M watching R fires once per flip.
origin: 0 0 0

  S minecraft:stone
  L minecraft:lever[face=floor,facing=east,powered=false]
  O minecraft:observer[facing=west,powered=false]
  P minecraft:sticky_piston[extended=false,facing=up]
  K minecraft:stone
  W minecraft:observer[facing=west,powered=false]
  A minecraft:stone
  R minecraft:repeater[delay=2,facing=west,locked=false,powered=false]
  F minecraft:stone
  M minecraft:observer[facing=west,powered=false]
