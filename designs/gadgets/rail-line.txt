# Powered rails as a zero-delay wire that powers nothing (PoweredRailBlock.updateState: a rail is
# powered by any neighbor signal, or by a powered rail of its line that is itself fed, searching
# at most 8 rails away; setBlockAndUpdate, so the next rail re-checks in the same tick).
# Lever L powers rail 1; rails 1..9 (the fed one + 8) light in the click's own tick, up the slope
# (rail 2 ascends east); rail 10 stays dark. Observer O above rail 9 pulses 2 gt after each
# edge. Piston Q is rail 5's floor: a powered rail standing on it, and in its quasi-connectivity
# cell, never powers it. Every rail needs a rigid floor (a slope also its high side), so a line
# costs 2 blocks per cell like dust, but it can pass next to pistons without powering them.
