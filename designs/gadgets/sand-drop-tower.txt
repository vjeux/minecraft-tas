# chain, which drags S2 and R3 down; the sand falls after them. Bottom-up lift: S1, S2, R3.
# Without the chain S1 could only pull S2 and R3 would stay one cell too high.
# Redstone blocks stand in for the control (the drive clears/sets them 4 gt apart; slower than
# ~4 gt the falling sand lands on a moving piston and breaks).
