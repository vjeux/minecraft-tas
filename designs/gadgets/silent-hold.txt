name: gadget-silent-hold
# Silent hold (BUD hold, found by the redstone-block track for rb5): piston P is powered only
# through its quasi cell (0,2,0) by block H, which repeater R switches. A repeater switching a
# solid block updates only that block's neighbors, and P is not one of them: H turning on does
# not extend P, H turning off does not retract it; P only re-checks when something next to it
# changes. Lever L drives R.
origin: 0 0 0
palette:
  P minecraft:sticky_piston[extended=false,facing=north]
  H minecraft:stone
  R minecraft:repeater[delay=1,facing=east,locked=false,powered=false]
  L minecraft:lever[face=floor,facing=east,powered=false]
  S minecraft:smooth_stone
layer 1:
  P.SS
layer 2:
  .HRL
