name: gadget-zero-tick-pulse
# A 0-tick POWER pulse and how components see it: redstone block R3 exists as a placed block only
# inside one block-event phase. T (sticky) 0-ticks it onto (-1,1,-2) as in zero-tick (X shoves the
# script's redstone block away); R3's placement updates Z (armed silently), which shoves R3 down
# in the same phase. Around (-1,1,-2): the repeater turns on 2 gt later and stays on for its delay
# (DiodeBlock.tick: it turns on, then schedules its own turn-off: stretched to 2 gt); the
# comparator sees nothing (ComparatorBlock recomputes its output in its tick, the input is gone:
# swallowed); the lamp lit at once and goes dark 4 gt later. S is obsidian at rest so that X,
# powered by R3 at rest, cannot extend before the script replaces S by a redstone block.
origin: -2 0 -3
palette:
  S minecraft:stone
  T minecraft:sticky_piston[extended=false,facing=north]
  X minecraft:piston[extended=false,facing=south]
  Z minecraft:piston[extended=false,facing=down]
  R minecraft:redstone_block
  O minecraft:obsidian
  Q minecraft:repeater[delay=1,facing=west,locked=false,powered=false]
  C minecraft:comparator[facing=south,mode=compare,powered=false]
  L minecraft:redstone_lamp[lit=false]
layer 0:
  .S
  ..S
layer 1:
  .C
  L.Q
  .RX
  .TO
layer 2:
  .
  .Z
