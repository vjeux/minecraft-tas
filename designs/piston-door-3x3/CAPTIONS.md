# How each design works, in one line

Read by `mctas door gallery` and `mctas door montage` (the caption under each GIF and clip).
A design missing here falls back to its row in the record progression of
[archive/README.md](archive/README.md). One row per archived design (`archive/<design>.txt`);
add a row when a new design tops a board.

| design | how it works |
|---|---|
| speed-wire-w2-139b | agent speed, built for ticks instead of blocks: the top-middle block leaves into its exit cell first, a pusher shoves a sticky puller into that exit, the puller and a fixed piston above it pull the center out in two quick moves (1-tick pulses), and the close runs it backwards with spits; opens in 12 ticks, closes in 14 |
| fc2-40b | agent fresh-c, "symmetric control": open and close run the SAME signal chain at the same delays, so there is no hold at all: an observer on the lever pulses the center piston A 2 ticks after each flip (swallowed on open, it pushes the center down on close), the shuttle piston Q is the lever through one repeater (its redstone block powers A at rest, then the right piston, which drags the top-middle block out), and the B window fires on both edges but is harmlessly blocked on close; fc2 makes the left pusher a pull-type pulse piston west of its backbone, fired by two observers off the lever (opens in 24 ticks, closes in 9) |
| fc1-42b | agent fresh-c, "symmetric control": the same signal chain at the same delays on both lever edges (A pulsed 2 ticks after each flip, Q = the lever through one repeater, a B window that is harmlessly blocked on close), so no hold at all |
| piston-door-3x3-fresh-b-mp8-43b | agent fresh-b, "mirrored pump": the bottom door row is glued to a slime row and pumped from below the door, the center exits upward, and the moves are triggered by the side structures' own motion (flags riding them) instead of delay lines; mp8's pump repeater fires the pump directly. Also the fastest solid door: opens and closes in 16 ticks |
| piston-door-3x3-fresh-b-mp7-45b | agent fresh-b: the mirrored pump (mp6) with a shorter pump path; opens and closes in 16 ticks |
| piston-door-3x3-fresh-b-mp6-46b | agent fresh-b, "mirrored pump": bottom row on a slime row pumped from below the door, the center exits upward, motion-triggered by flags on the side structures; opens and closes in 16 ticks |
| fresh-b-mp6-45b | agent fresh-b: a 45-block variant of the mirrored pump mp6 |
| riders-r2-46b | agent riders: rb6-bmobs (47) without its 7-block silent hold: a redstone block riding the left structure lands under the shuttle piston Q while the left column is open and keeps it powered; the left piston is held by a second B-window repeater, so the left structure only comes back after the close dance (closes in 23 ticks instead of 25) |
| slime-free-combo-48b | agent slime-free, no slime or honey anywhere: logic-min's slime-free 50 (each side column on sticky pistons retracted at rest, fired by observer pulses; the right column fed by an observer + dust; a torch quasi-powers the top piston) combined with this track's left side, where the top-left door block goes UP into a puller fired by an observer chain, and a B-chain link re-grown by `door grow` |
| gravity-g10-45b | agent gravity, all 9 door blocks are sand: side columns on slime backbones pushed open by the doorway-floor pistons; the middle column stands on a 3-piston tower in a shaft under the door. | mechanics-fast-57b | agent mechanics, fastest solid door (open 16, close 17): mechanics' 47 (bottom-middle piston fired by an observer) plus redstone-block rb9's second-pulse route (an observer on the lever and a delay line into R_B: B moves twice, at ticks 4..6 and 8..12); the minimizer's swaps then turned three dust + floor pairs into single blocks |
| logic-min-slime-free-50b | agent logic-min, no slime or honey anywhere: each side column stands on 3 sticky pistons, retracted at rest and fired by 2-tick observer pulses (a short pulse pulls a block on open, pushes it back and lets go on close); the right observer fires straight into the right pistons' dust (on close the top piston and the left column move once more before settling) |
| st43-bmobs-42b | agent top-control on search-tool's 43: the redstone-block family with a sculk sensor and one repeater as the late hold, and the bottom-middle piston retracted at rest, fired by an observer watching the bottom-right door block |
| gravity-g6-49b | agent gravity, all 9 door blocks are sand: the side columns ride slime backbones pushed open by sticky pistons that are the doorway floor; the middle column stands on a 3-piston tower in a shaft under the door that collapses top-down (the sand drops 3 cells) and lifts it back bottom-up; g6 hangs the first torch under a dust (-2 from g4) |
