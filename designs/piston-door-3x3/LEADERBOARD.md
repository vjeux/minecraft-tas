# 3x3 piston door leaderboards

Generated by `mctas door leaderboard`; never edit it by hand. Every design on main and on the `explore/*` branches, judged once by main's verifier (3 open/close cycles; new designs at 32 positions, older verdicts at up to 32). Each board shows its leader (GIF from `door gallery`, video, how it works; a door leading several boards is shown once); click "more" for the rest. What every board means and the rules: [docs/rules.md](../../docs/rules.md). Every archived design with its video: [archive/README.md](archive/README.md). The story: [docs/history.md](../../docs/history.md).

**Size = volume**, as the piston door community counts a door's blocks (its regulation, section 4.2): the bounding box of the build over every state of a cycle (at rest, every tick of the opening and the closing, open), the lever (the input device) excluded. A door listed as `80b (8x10x1)` fits in 8 wide x 10 high x 1 deep, however many blocks it places. Every size board ranks by it, then by blocks placed (non-air blocks, piston heads excluded; the `blocks` column), then by open + close ticks. Measured once per design (`door properties`, cached in `archive/properties-v1.tsv`; definition: [tracks/props/NOTES.md](tracks/props/NOTES.md)). Archive file names keep the placed-block count they were archived with (`fc2-40b`): they are IDs, not sizes.

Walkway "clean" = nothing in the 3-deep walkway at rest (designs judged before the 2026-10-18 16:40Z "nothing in front of or behind the door" rule keep their verdicts). The boards rank rotation-safe designs; the others are on [Not rotation-safe](#not-rotation-safe).
