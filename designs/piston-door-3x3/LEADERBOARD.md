# 3x3 piston door leaderboards

Generated by `mctas door leaderboard`; never edit it by hand. Every design on main and on the `explore/*` branches, judged by main's verifier (3 open/close cycles at 32 positions). Each board shows its leader (GIF from `door gallery`, video, how it works); click "more" for the rest. What every board means and the rules: [docs/rules.md](../../docs/rules.md). Every archived design with its video: [archive/README.md](archive/README.md). The story: [docs/history.md](../../docs/history.md).

Walkway "clean" = nothing in the 3-deep walkway at rest (designs judged before the 2026-10-18 16:40Z "nothing in front of or behind the door" rule keep their verdicts). The boards rank rotation-safe designs; the others are on [Not rotation-safe](#not-rotation-safe).
