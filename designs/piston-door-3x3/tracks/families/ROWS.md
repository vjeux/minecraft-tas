# Strip doors: row (and column) mechanisms

Generated by `mctas door families --rows` (crates/piston-door/src/families.rs). Do not edit by hand.

In a box of height 3, 4 or 5 (width 3, 4 or 5 for columns) the door blocks can only slide sideways in their rows (up/down in their columns): nothing can pull a block out of the 3x3 vertically. Bounds: each door row is solved on its own (pistons facing E/W in the row, no vertical pistons), EVERY placement of at most **5 pistons** (sticky or normal, retracted or extended at rest) in the row's arms of at most 5 cells each, no helpers, no slime; solved exactly in the macro model (pulse model state graph): the 3 door cells empty and the 2 ring cells of the row holding only air or door blocks when open (FULL seamless; the top and bottom ring rows are outside a height-3 box, the hallway fills them), back to the exact rest state, every state inside the row box. Minimal (no piston can be dropped), mirror images merged. A door = one row mechanism per row (any three, each also mirrored): a W x 3 box with arms a + b holds every row mechanism that fits a + b. A column mechanism is a row mechanism turned 90 degrees (a 3 x H box; up/down is not a symmetry for the wiring: both turns count).
